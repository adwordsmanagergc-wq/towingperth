## Vehicle transport across Perth

Not every tow starts with a breakdown or a crash. Plenty of the cars on our trucks are perfectly fine, they just need to get from one place to another without being driven. Maybe they're unregistered, maybe they've been sitting for years or maybe the owner would simply rather not put kilometres on them.

Typical jobs include collecting a car bought at auction, picking up a private sale from the other side of the city, moving a project car to a panel shop or engine builder, delivering a car to its new owner after a sale, and shifting vehicles when someone moves house. We move cars through to larger vehicles, and with more than 10 trucks working across Perth, it's usually easy to fit a booking around your plans.

## Tilt tray transport for every kind of vehicle

**Tilt tray, not towing on the road.** Every vehicle we move rides fully on the tray. There's no wear on the tyres, gearbox or driveline, and no extra kilometres on the odometer. That matters for automatics, all-wheel-drive cars and anything valuable.

**Non-running cars.** A car that hasn't started in years can still be moved. We winch it onto the tray slowly and secure it for the trip. Let us know about flat tyres, seized brakes or missing parts so we come prepared.

**Auction, dealer and private sale pickups.** Buying a car you can't drive home is common. It may be unregistered, a long way from your place or simply not roadworthy yet. We collect from auction houses, dealer yards and private driveways. Have the paperwork ready and tell the seller when to expect us.

**Project and classic cars.** Restorations move around a lot: from shed to panel shop, to the painter, to the trimmer and back again. We load these cars carefully, watching clearances on low bodywork and securing them in a way that protects paint and trim. If the car is in pieces, tell us what's attached and what's loose.

**Moving house.** When you move, the car you don't drive often gets left until last. Whether it's a second car without rego, a project in the garage or a vehicle you can't drive yourself, we can move it to the new address so you have one less thing to organise.

If the car has been damaged in a collision, see our [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) page.
