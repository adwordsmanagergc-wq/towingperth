## What actually decides the cost of a tow

Every tow in Perth is priced on a handful of practical things. None of them are hidden, and we will walk you through them on the phone if you want. Here is what we look at.

**Distance.** This covers how far the truck travels to reach you and how far your vehicle goes after that. A breakdown in [Joondalup](/areas/joondalup) going to a workshop down the road is a very different job to a car in Two Rocks heading to a repairer in [Rockingham](/areas/rockingham).

**Vehicle type and size.** A small hatch, a dual cab ute, a lowered sports car, an EV and a loaded 4WD all need different handling. Weight, length, ground clearance and drive type decide how we load it on the tilt tray and what straps and gear we use.

**Running or not.** If the car rolls, steers and brakes, loading is quick. If it has seized wheels, a locked steering column, no keys or flat batteries, we need more time and sometimes skates or extra winching.

## Access, timing and waiting

**Access at both ends.** A car on a flat street is easy. A car in an underground car park with low clearance, down a battleaxe driveway, on a hills block in Kalamunda or stuck behind a locked gate takes more work. The drop off point matters too, so tell us if the workshop is closed or the destination has tight access.

**Time of day and urgency.** We run 24/7, including weekends and public holidays. A non-urgent job booked ahead can usually be slotted into a truck's day. An immediate callout late at night on a public holiday is a different job.

**Waiting time.** If the driver has to wait for police, a tenant to move cars, a gate to be opened or paperwork to be signed, that time can be part of the quote. Let us know about anything that might hold things up.

## Straight tow vs recovery work

A straight tow is a vehicle on a hard surface that can go straight onto the tray. Recovery is anything that has to be done before that: pulling a car out of a ditch on [Wanneroo Road](/roads/wanneroo-road), winching a 4WD out of soft sand, or righting a vehicle after a crash. Recovery needs more time, gear and care, so it is priced separately. See [4WD recovery](/services/4wd-recovery-perth) for off road jobs.

## Insurance often means nothing on the day

If your car was in an accident and you are covered, we can bill your insurer directly. In most cases that means you pay nothing on the day and the car goes to your repairer or your insurer's approved repairer. Read more on our [insurance towing](/insurance-towing) page.
