## A tow partner your workshop can rely on

Every workshop needs a tow operator they can trust. Customers break down and ask you who to call. Cars need to come in for diagnosis and go out to specialists. Sometimes a finished job needs to go home because the owner is stuck at work.

We work with mechanics across Perth to handle those moves. We answer 24/7, give a realistic ETA from the nearest of our 10+ trucks, and every car goes on a tilt tray with no wear on the tyres, gearbox or driveline.

## Jobs we do for workshops

- **Breakdown collections.** A customer's car dies on the [Mitchell Freeway](/roads/mitchell-freeway) or in their driveway. They call you, you call us, and the car turns up at your door.
- **Customer referrals.** Give your customers our number and tell them to send the car to you. We quote them directly and drop it at your workshop.
- **Specialist runs.** Moving cars to and from auto electricians, transmission specialists, tyre shops and panel shops.
- **Return deliveries.** Taking repaired cars home when the owner cannot collect, or moving cars that are not safe to drive.
- **Overflow and storage.** Shifting cars to a second site or storage yard when your floor is full.

## After-hours drop offs

A lot of breakdowns happen after you have shut the roller door. We can still deliver the car so it is ready for you in the morning. Tell us once where you want after-hours cars left, how the keys should be handled, and whether there is a gate code or compound. We will note it and follow it each time.

The driver will park the car where it will not block your bays or your neighbours, leave a note with the customer's name and details if you want, and photograph the car at drop off on request.

## Handling customer cars with care

When a customer's car arrives at your shop, it reflects on you. Our drivers strap at the wheels, check clearance before loading lowered cars, and handle AWD vehicles and EVs on the tray so the drivetrain is not turned. If a car has a fault that affects loading, like seized brakes or a locked steering column, we bring the right gear.

## What to tell us when you book

- the customer's name and contact number
- where the car is and where it is going
- make, model and whether it rolls and steers
- where keys are
- any after-hours drop off instructions
- whether to invoice the workshop or the customer

## Pricing

Each job is quoted on distance, vehicle type, whether the car runs, access at both ends and time of day or urgency. You or your customer get a fixed upfront price with no hidden fees and our best rates guarantee. For insured accident cars, we can bill the [insurer directly](/insurance-towing).
