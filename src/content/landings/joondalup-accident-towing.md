## Crashes on Joondalup's main roads

Joondalup Drive, Ocean Reef Road and Shenton Avenue carry heavy traffic through the city centre, the health campus and out to the coast. Rear-enders at the lights, side-swipes at roundabouts and collisions at busy intersections like Joondalup Drive and Hodges Drive are common.

On the [Mitchell Freeway](/roads/mitchell-freeway) near the Joondalup and Hodges Drive exits, crashes happen at speed and in fast traffic. If you're stuck on the freeway, stay behind the barrier if there is one and away from the car, and wait for help.

Whatever the location, we send the nearest truck with a realistic ETA, 24/7.

## Lakeside Joondalup car park bumps

Not every accident is on the open road. Car park scrapes and low speed collisions at Lakeside Joondalup can leave a car with a broken wheel, a smashed radiator or a bumper dragging on the ground. It might look minor, but if the car isn't safe to drive, don't risk it.

Tell us which car park and level you're in, and whether the car can roll and steer. Multi-level car parks have height limits, so we plan the pickup to suit.

## Loading a damaged car

Every accident tow is done on a tilt tray. The car rides fully on the tray, not on its own wheels, so damaged suspension, a flat tyre or a bent rim won't make things worse on the trip. Cars that can't roll are winched up carefully.

Loose parts like bumpers, mirrors and trim are collected and secured with the car where possible. Debris from the crash is cleared so the road can reopen.

## Insurance and where your car goes

For covered claims, we bill your insurer directly, so there's usually nothing for you to pay on the day. We take the car to your chosen repairer or to your insurer's approved repairer. If you're not sure yet, we can take it somewhere safe and move it once your claim is sorted. Read more on [insurance towing](/insurance-towing).

## What to do while you wait

- Check everyone is okay. Call 000 if anyone is hurt or the road is blocked
- Turn on your hazard lights and set up a warning triangle if you carry one
- Swap names, phone numbers, rego and insurance details with the other driver
- Take photos of the damage, the cars' positions and the street or exit
- Remove valuables, documents and car seats if it's safe to do so
