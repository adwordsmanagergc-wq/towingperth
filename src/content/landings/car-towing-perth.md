## Car towing in Perth, done on a tilt tray

Car towing Perth drivers can trust starts with the right truck. Every car we move rides fully on a tilt tray: the tray slides back to the ground, the car is winched or driven on, and it travels strapped down with no wheels on the road. Nothing is dragged, no kilometres are added and the gearbox never turns. That matters for automatics, all wheel drives, hybrids and EVs, where towing on two wheels can do expensive damage.

We are Quik Tow & Transport, based in Osborne Park, and we have been towing cars around Perth for more than 25 years. More than 10 trucks work across the metro area every day, so we send the nearest one rather than whoever is free across town.

## What to have ready when you call

To give you a fixed quote quickly, we ask:

1. Where the car is now, including the car park level or bay if it is in a centre.

3. The make and model, and anything unusual such as lowered suspension, a lift kit or an EV.
