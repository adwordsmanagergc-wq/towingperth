## Why unregistered cars need to be carried

Once a car's rego lapses, it cannot legally be driven on WA roads in the normal way. That catches a lot of people out: you buy a car without rego, let the rego lapse while it sat in the garage, or need to get it inspected before re-licensing. Driving it "just around the corner" can mean a fine, and it is unlikely to be insured.

The simple fix is to carry it. On a tilt tray, the car rides fully on the deck. It is cargo, not a vehicle being driven, so rego is not needed for the trip. Because the car never touches the bitumen, there is no tyre or driveline wear and the odometer stays put.

## Getting the timing right

If you have a booked inspection time, tell us when you call. We will plan the pickup so the car arrives with time to spare. If the car needs to stay overnight at a mechanic before the inspection, check that the workshop is happy to hold it.

Keep any paperwork with you or in the car: proof of ownership, any previous rego papers, and the inspection booking details. That way the car and paperwork arrive together.
