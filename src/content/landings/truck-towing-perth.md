## Truck towing in Perth, from light rigid to heavy

A truck that dies on the Roe Highway or in a loading dock costs money every hour it sits there. Our truck towing service gets it off the road and to your workshop, depot or dealer, 24 hours a day. We have been towing in Perth for more than 25 years from our Osborne Park base, and our fleet of more than 10 trucks covers everything from tilt trays for light trucks to heavier units for bigger vehicles.

Every heavy job is different, so we ask the right questions first: the make and model, the gross vehicle mass, the wheelbase, whether it rolls and steers, and whether the air brakes will release. That lets us send the right truck the first time rather than finding out at the roadside.

## Heavy towing Perth businesses rely on

Most of our heavy towing work comes from businesses:

- light and medium rigid trucks, tippers and tray trucks
- delivery trucks, pantechs and refrigerated bodies
- service trucks, cherry pickers and truck mounted equipment
- tow vehicles and support trucks from the mining and construction trades
- utes and vans loaded with tools (see [ute and van towing](/towing/van-towing-perth))

For plant and machinery up to 11 tonnes, such as bobcats, mini excavators and forklifts, see [machinery transport](/transport/machinery-transport-perth). For regular business work, set up an account through [fleet towing](/business/fleet-towing-perth).

## Bus towing service

We tow school buses, minibuses, community and charter buses, coaches and motorhomes. Buses are long, heavy and often have low skirts or air suspension, so the details matter. Tell us the length, the GVM and whether the air system holds pressure. A bus that has lost air may need its brakes caged before it can be moved. If passengers are on board, get everyone off and well away from traffic before anything else.

## Where trucks break down in Perth

The calls we get for trucks cluster around the freight routes and industrial areas:

**Freeways and highways.** The Kwinana and Mitchell freeways, Roe Highway, Tonkin Highway, Great Eastern Highway and Leach Highway carry most of Perth's freight. If you are stopped on one, see our [freeway breakdown](/towing/freeway-breakdown-perth) safety steps.

**Industrial estates.** We work every week in [Kewdale](/areas/kewdale), [Welshpool](/areas/welshpool), [Wangara](/areas/wangara), [Malaga](/areas/malaga) and the [Kwinana](/areas/kwinana) industrial strip, moving trucks between depots, workshops and dealers.

**After a crash.** If a truck has been in an accident we can clear it once police allow it, and for covered claims we bill the insurer directly. See [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth).

## What to tell us when you call

1. Where the truck or bus is, and which way it is facing if it is on a highway.
2. The make, model, GVM and length.
3. What failed, whether it rolls, and whether the brakes release.
4. Whether there is a load on board.
5. Where it needs to go, and any access limits at the drop off, such as low gates or tight yards.
