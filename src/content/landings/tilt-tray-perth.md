## Tilt tray towing in Perth

A tilt tray truck has a flat bed that slides back and tilts down to the ground. The vehicle is winched or driven up, strapped at the wheels and carried with nothing on the road. It is the safest way to move almost any car, and it is how we do nearly every job.

We have been running tilt trays around Perth for more than 25 years, from our base in Osborne Park. More than 10 trucks work across the city, so for most tilt tray jobs there is one not too far away, day or night.

## What a tilt tray can carry

- cars, utes and 4WDs, running or not (see [car towing](/car-towing-perth))
- lowered, prestige and classic cars, loaded at a low angle
- electric cars and hybrids, with all wheels off the ground
- motorbikes, quad bikes and ride on mowers
- bobcats, mini excavators, forklifts and other plant up to 11 tonnes (see [machinery transport](/transport/machinery-transport-perth))
- small boats on trailers, garden sheds, pallets and site equipment
- non running project cars and deceased estate vehicles (see [non running car transport](/transport/non-running-car-transport-perth))

For shipping containers, our [container towing](/services/container-transport-perth) page explains the trucks and site checks involved. For trucks and buses, see [truck towing](/truck-towing-perth).
