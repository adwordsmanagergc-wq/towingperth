## Cheap should still mean done properly

Most people searching for cheap towing in Perth do not want the lowest number on a screen. They want a fair price, a truck that turns up, and no surprises when the job is finished. That is what we focus on.

Every quote is given upfront, before the truck is dispatched. There are no hidden fees, no "extras" added at the drop off, and our best rates guarantee sits behind the price. If something about the job would change the cost, we tell you before we start, not after.

All of our work is done on tilt tray trucks. Your car sits fully on the tray, so there is no wear on the tyres, gearbox or driveline and no kilometres added. That matters for automatics, AWD vehicles and EVs, where towing on the wheels can cause expensive damage.

## Ways to keep your towing cost down

You have more control over the price than you might think. These simple steps can make a real difference.

**Book ahead for non-urgent jobs.** Moving a non-running project car, taking a car to a mechanic, or collecting a private sale vehicle rarely needs a truck straight away. Give us a window and we can fit it in efficiently.

**Make the car easy to reach.** Move other cars out of the driveway, open gates, and clear the area around the vehicle. If it is in a shopping centre car park like Westfield Carousel or Karrinyup, note the level and bay number. Less time spent getting to the car means a simpler job.

**Have the keys ready.** A car with keys can usually be put in neutral and steered. Without keys, the steering may lock and the job gets harder.

**Check if it rolls.** Tell us if the handbrake releases and the wheels turn. Seized brakes or flat tyres change how we load.

**Check your cover.** Look at your roadside assistance membership and your car insurance before you book. Many policies include towing. For accident claims see our [insurance towing](/insurance-towing) page.

**Be specific when you call.** The clearer the details, the more accurate the quote, and the less chance of the job running over.
