## Why tilt tray matters for mechanical faults

With a tilt tray, the car rides fully on the deck. The wheels do not turn, so a failing gearbox, CV joint, diff or wheel bearing is not loaded up on the way. No kilometres are added either. For automatics, AWDs and EVs, that avoids the damage that dragging a car on its wheels can cause.

## Workshop hours and timing

Most Perth workshops work business hours, Monday to Friday, with some open Saturday mornings. Plan around that:

- **During hours:** we can deliver straight to the workshop bay and hand over keys in person.
- **After hours:** if the mechanic allows it, we can drop the car in an agreed spot and leave the keys in their key drop box. Get the details from them first.
- **Scheduled tows:** if the car is safe at home, we can book a time that lines up with the workshop opening.

We are available 24/7, so the tow can happen whenever suits you. The workshop's rules decide where and when the car can be left.

## Tell the mechanic it is coming

A short call to your mechanic saves a lot of hassle. Let them know:

- When the car is arriving and who is bringing it.
- What the fault is, or what you have already been told.
- Where they want it parked and how to leave the keys.
- Your best contact number.

Some workshops in industrial areas like [Osborne Park](/areas/osborne-park), [Malaga](/areas/malaga), [Wangara](/areas/wangara) and [Welshpool](/areas/welshpool) have tight yards, so knowing in advance helps them make space.
