## Containers for new builds in Baldivis

Baldivis has grown fast, with new estates and house lots going in across the suburb. That brings a lot of container work: builders needing a lockable site container for tools and materials, owners wanting secure storage while they build, and families moving in who need a spot for gear until the garage is sorted.

We deliver 10, 20 and 40 ft containers to lots and homes across Baldivis, and move them again when the job is done.

## Lot access in new estates

New estates have their own access challenges. Streets may be partly built, with kerbs, footpaths and service pits going in. Neighbouring lots may already have slabs, frames or fencing, which narrows the space for the truck to line up.

The truck needs to reverse in straight, tilt the tray and drive forward as the container slides off. Before we book, we check the route into the lot, the width of the access, and whether builders' gear, skip bins or materials are in the way. If the estate has a site office or access rules, let us know.

## Soft verges and sand pads

The ground in Baldivis is mostly sand. Sand pads on new lots and unfinished verges can be soft, especially if they've been disturbed by machinery or recently watered by reticulation. A loaded truck can sink if the ground can't take the weight.

Where possible, we place the container on firmer, compacted ground away from service pits, kerbs and new footpaths. Level pads or sleepers under each corner help keep the container straight and the doors working. If the lot is too soft on the day, we'll tell you rather than risk bogging the truck or damaging the kerb.

## Site containers for builders

If you're a builder or trade working several lots, we can deliver containers to new sites, move them between lots as stages finish, and collect them at handover. Tell us the order of the jobs and we'll plan the moves to suit. Let us know if a container is loaded, as weight affects how it's handled.

## What to tell us when you call

- Lot number, street and estate name, plus a pin if the street is new
- Container size and whether it's empty or loaded
- Where on the lot you want it, and which way the doors should face
- What's already on site: slab, frame, fencing, skip bins
- Ground conditions and any soft or wet areas
