## When it can't wait

Some tows can be booked for tomorrow. Others can't. A car stopped in a live lane on the Kwinana Freeway, a crash at a busy intersection, a car that has died in a tunnel or a work ute that won't start with a crew waiting on it all need a truck now.

That is what our emergency line is for. Ring 0419 857 070 at any hour and a real person answers. We take your location, check which of our trucks is closest and send it straight to you.

## Our crash towing authorisation

Quik Tow & Transport is a WA Department of Transport authorised crash towing business, authorisation #810. Since 1 July 2026, only authorised businesses can tow a crashed vehicle in Perth, and the charges for a crash tow are set by regulation. You can check the [regulated crash towing charges](#regulated-crash-towing) on this page, so you know what to expect before you agree to anything at the scene.

## Stay safe until we arrive

- Turn on your hazard lights and, if the car can move, get it as far off the road as you safely can.
- On a freeway or highway, get out on the side away from traffic and wait behind the barrier.
- If you feel unsafe in a quiet spot at night, lock the doors and stay inside until our truck pulls up.
- Take photos of any crash damage and swap details with the other driver while you wait.

## Tell us these four things

1. Where you are. A street address, freeway exit, landmark or a map pin sent by text all work.
2. What you drive, and whether it is an auto, manual, AWD or electric.
3. What happened: crash damage, won't start, overheated, flat with no spare.
4. Where the car needs to go: home, your mechanic, a repairer or your insurer's yard.
