## Fleet downtime costs money

For a business running vehicles, a breakdown is more than a towing bill. A courier van off the road means missed deliveries. A rental car stuck at the airport means an unhappy customer. A tradie's ute in a ditch means a crew standing around on site.

We help Perth fleets get vehicles moving again. We answer 24/7, dispatch the nearest of our 10+ trucks and give a realistic ETA, so your team can plan around it.

## Who we tow for

- **Rental and car share companies.** Recovering broken down hire cars, moving cars between branches and the airport, and getting damaged vehicles to repairers.
- **Couriers and delivery fleets.** Towing vans that break down mid-route so the driver can transfer the load and keep going.
- **Councils and government fleets.** Towing pool cars, utes and light vehicles to workshops and depots.
- **Trades fleets.** Recovering utes and vans fitted with racks, toolboxes and canopies, from job sites, freeways and depots.
- **Company car pools.** Breakdowns, accident tows and workshop runs for staff cars.

## Consistent service for every vehicle

Fleet managers want the same result every time. Every vehicle goes on a tilt tray, so there is no wear on tyres, gearbox or driveline and no kilometres added. This matters for AWD vehicles, automatics and EVs, which can be damaged if towed on their wheels.

Our drivers follow your instructions on where to deliver, who to contact and how to handle keys. If you have a preferred workshop or repairer, we take the vehicle there.

## Invoicing to your business

Rather than drivers paying on a personal card and claiming it back, we can invoice your business directly. Each job can carry the rego, driver name and pickup and drop off points so it matches your records. For accident claims, we can bill the [insurer directly](/insurance-towing).

## Setting up fleet towing

Talk to us about how your fleet operates. Useful details include:

- the types of vehicles and any fit-outs or loads
- the depots, branches or yards they run from
- your preferred workshops and repairers
- who approves jobs and who should get updates
- how you want invoices set out

Once we have this, your drivers can call one number and the job runs the way you want it.

## What your drivers should do while waiting

Tell your drivers to get to a safe spot, put hazard lights on and stay well clear of traffic. On busy roads like the [Mitchell Freeway](/roads/mitchell-freeway), [Great Eastern Highway](/roads/great-eastern-highway) or [Albany Highway](/roads/albany-highway), they should wait behind a barrier if possible. When they call, they should give the exact location, vehicle rego and what happened.

## Pricing

Fleet jobs are quoted on distance, vehicle type and size, whether it runs, access at both ends and time of day or urgency. You get upfront quotes with no hidden fees and our best rates guarantee. Call or text to talk about fleet work.
