## The short answer

Call roadside assistance when the car can probably be fixed where it is and you are a member. Call a tow truck when it can't be fixed on the spot, when it has been in a crash, when you have no roadside cover, or when you want it taken to a particular place. We are a towing company, so we would rather tell you this straight than have you pay for a tow you did not need.

## When roadside assistance is the better choice

If you are a member of a roadside service like [RAC WA](https://rac.com.au/) and the problem is simple, call them first. A patrol can often jump start a battery, swap a tyre or bring fuel, and you drive away without needing a tow at all. That is usually included in what you already pay for.

## When to call a tow truck instead

**The car can't be fixed where it is.** A failed starter, a cooked engine, a snapped belt or a gearbox fault usually means the car has to go to a workshop. If your roadside provider has already been and the car still won't go, a tow is the next step. See [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth).

**There has been a crash.** In WA, only an authorised crash towing business can tow a crashed car from the scene, and the charges are capped by the WA Government. We are authorised crash tow business #810 and can bill your insurer directly. See [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth).

**You have no roadside cover.** Call or text 0419 857 070, tell us where you are and what you drive, and we give you a fixed price before the truck leaves.

**You want the car taken somewhere specific.** Your own mechanic across town, your home, or a dealer. We tow anywhere in Perth and quote the whole trip upfront. See [car towing](/car-towing-perth).

**You are somewhere unsafe.** On a freeway shoulder or a blind corner, the priority is getting the car off the road. See our [freeway breakdown](/towing/freeway-breakdown-perth) safety steps, and tell whoever you call that you are in a dangerous spot.

## Check what your cover includes

Roadside memberships and car insurance policies differ a lot. Before you agree to a destination, check whether towing is included, how far, and to where.
