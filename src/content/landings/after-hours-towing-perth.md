## A real person on the phone, any hour

Breaking down after dark feels different. Fewer people around, less light, and often a flat phone battery to worry about. When you ring 0419 857 070 at night, a real person picks up, takes your location and tells you which truck is coming.

We have 10+ tow trucks on the road across Perth, so we send the nearest one rather than whoever is free across town. You get a realistic ETA, not a guess designed to keep you on the line.

## Staying safe while you wait

Your safety comes before the car. If you can, get the vehicle off the road or as far left as possible, switch on your hazard lights and leave your parking lights on.

- If you are on a busy road or freeway, get out on the side away from traffic and wait behind a barrier if there is one.
- If you are in a quiet street or car park and feel unsafe, lock the doors and wait inside until you see our truck.
- Keep your phone charged. Turn down the screen brightness and close apps you are not using.
- Do not accept help from anyone claiming to be your tow truck. Our driver will know your name and job details. Ask them before you hand over keys.

If you are injured or in danger, call 000 first. Then call us.

## What to tell us when you ring

The more we know up front, the quicker we can get the right truck to you:

- Your exact location. A street address, nearest cross street, freeway exit or a GPS pin sent by text all help.
- Make, model and whether it is a manual, auto, AWD or EV.
- What happened: will not start, overheated, flat tyre with no spare, accident damage.
- Where you want the car to go, and whether anyone will be there to receive it.

If you have had an accident and are claiming on insurance, tell us your insurer. We can [bill your insurer directly](/insurance-towing) for covered claims, so there is usually nothing to pay on the night.

## How your car is loaded

We use tilt tray trucks. The tray tilts down, your car is winched or driven on, and it rides fully on the tray. No wheels on the road, no wear on the tyres, gearbox or driveline, and no kilometres added. That matters for AWDs, automatics and EVs, which can be damaged by being dragged.

For a standard [breakdown tow](/services/breakdown-towing-perth), loading usually takes a few minutes once the truck is in position. For [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth), the driver also secures loose panels and checks for leaking fluids before moving off.
