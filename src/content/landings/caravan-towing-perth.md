## Caravans that won't move

A caravan can stop being towable for plenty of reasons: a collapsed wheel bearing, flat tyres after months in storage, a broken coupling, seized electric brakes or a failed breakaway system. Sometimes the van is fine but the tow vehicle isn't. Either way, you need it moved without making the damage worse.

If you're on the side of the road, keep the caravan hitched to the car if it's safe, chock the van's wheels and put your hazards and warning triangle out. Don't try to unhitch on a slope or a soft shoulder. Get everyone away from traffic and call us.

## Tell us the length, weight and ATM

Caravans range from small pop tops to large tandem axle off road vans, so the details matter. Before we send a truck we'll ask for:

- Overall length, including the drawbar
- The ATM from the compliance plate, and how loaded it is now (water tanks, gas, gear)
- Single or tandem axle
- Whether the wheels turn and the brakes release
- Any crash or chassis damage

With that information we can confirm the right truck for the job, rather than turning up and having to come back.

## Storage, servicing and moves

Not every caravan job is an emergency. We move vans between home and storage yards, to service centres and repairers, to and from sale yards and for people moving house who no longer have a vehicle big enough to tow. If the van is coming out of a tight backyard or a storage facility with narrow aisles, describe the access when you call so we can plan how to get it out.

## Damaged caravans after a crash

Caravans are lightly built compared to cars, and after a rollover or a jackknife the walls, floor and chassis can be badly twisted. Loose fittings, gas bottles and water tanks all need to be checked before moving. Turn off the gas at the bottle if you can do it safely. We take care to secure the van so nothing shifts further during the trip, and can take it to your repairer or the insurer's approved repairer.
