## Moving dealer stock across Perth

Car yards move vehicles constantly. Stock heads between sister yards, trade-ins come off customers' driveways, cars go to detailers before photos and to workshops for roadworthy and service work. Using your sales staff to drive them wastes their time and puts kilometres on the stock.

We handle those moves on tilt trays. The car rides fully on the tray, so the odometer does not change and there is no wear on tyres, gearbox or driveline. That keeps your stock presentable and your listings accurate.

## Typical car yard jobs

- **Yard to yard transfers.** Moving stock between dealerships in places like [Osborne Park](/areas/osborne-park), [Cannington](/areas/cannington), [Wangara](/areas/wangara) or [Midland](/areas/midland).
- **Trade-in collections.** Picking up a customer's trade-in from their home or workplace, running or not.
- **Detailers and paint shops.** Getting cars to and from detailers, paint and dent repairers and window tinters.
- **Workshops.** Delivering vehicles for servicing, roadworthy inspections and mechanical repairs, then bringing them back.
- **Customer deliveries.** Delivering a sold car to a buyer's home when they cannot collect.
- **Auction runs.** Taking cars to or from auction houses such as Pickles, Manheim and Grays.

## Careful loading for retail stock

Cars on your lot are worth more when they look perfect. Our drivers treat every vehicle as retail stock. We strap at the wheels, not through the body, check clearance on lowered and sports models before loading, and take extra care with low front bumpers and side skirts.

Before we load, the driver will walk around the car. If there are existing marks you want noted, point them out. At the drop off, the car is unloaded the same way it went on.

## Regular and multi-car bookings

Most dealers want one number to call that gets things moving. Once we know your yards and your usual destinations, booking is quick. You can send a list of cars and locations by text, and we will schedule the trucks.

For bigger moves, like clearing a batch of trade-ins or shifting stock for a sale weekend, give us notice and we will plan it across our fleet. We can invoice the business so your accounts team is not chasing receipts.

## What to tell us when you book

- each vehicle's make, model and rego or stock number
- pickup and drop off addresses, including the contact on site
- whether each car runs and where keys will be left
- any access limits, like yard opening times or tight driveways
- whether any car is lowered, modified or an EV
