## Albany towing, run from Perth

We want to be clear up front: Quik Tow & Transport is a Perth towing company, based in Osborne Park, and we do not have a truck stationed in Albany. What we do offer is booked tilt tray runs between Perth and Albany, for vehicles that need to make the trip on a tray rather than on their own wheels.

Albany is around 420 km from Perth down [Albany Highway](/roads/albany-highway), roughly a five hour drive each way. We have been doing long runs like this for more than 25 years, so we know how to plan them properly.

## When people book an Albany run

- **A car that broke down on a trip south.** It has been towed to a local yard or workshop in Albany, and now it needs to come home to Perth.
- **A car bought in Albany.** A private sale or dealer purchase that needs delivering to a Perth address.
- **Moving to or from the Great Southern.** A second car, a project car or a car that is not registered.
- **Caravans and campers.** A van that needs to come back to Perth for repairs. See [caravan towing](/towing/caravan-towing-perth).
- **Non running vehicles.** Cars that have sat for years, deceased estate cars and project builds. See [non running car transport](/transport/non-running-car-transport-perth).

## Tow truck Albany: what to do in an emergency

If you have just broken down or crashed in or around Albany and need to be moved off the road now, call a local Albany tow operator or your roadside assistance first. A truck coming from Perth cannot get to you as fast as someone local. Once the car is safe, call us on 0419 857 070 and we will quote the trip back to Perth.

## How an Albany to Perth run works

1. Call or text with the vehicle, both addresses and whether it runs.
2. We give you a fixed price for the whole trip and agree a day.
3. Our driver collects the car on a tilt tray, strapped at the wheels.
4. We deliver it to your home, mechanic or repairer in Perth, or the other way round.
