## Breaking down around Midland

Midland sits where several major roads meet, and that's where a lot of breakdowns happen. Overheating on the climb up Great Eastern Highway towards the hills, flat batteries after a cold night, failed fuel pumps and blown tyres are all common.

On the [Great Eastern Highway](/roads/great-eastern-highway), the Great Eastern Bypass and Roe Highway, traffic moves fast and heavy vehicles are everywhere. Stopping on the shoulder is risky. We send the nearest truck with a realistic ETA, 24/7.

## Roe Highway and the Bypass

Roe Highway runs north to south through the area, and the Great Eastern Bypass carries a lot of freight traffic heading east. If your car loses power on either, try to coast to the far left shoulder or an exit before you stop. Once you've stopped, turn on your hazards, get out on the side away from traffic if it's safe, and wait behind the barrier.

Tell us which direction you were travelling and the nearest exit or cross street, such as Toodyay Road, Clayton Street or Reid Highway. It helps the driver find you quickly.

## Midland Gate and local streets

Not every breakdown happens at speed. Cars fail to start in the Midland Gate car park, outside the train station or on side streets in Midvale, Woodbridge and Swan View. These jobs are about access: car park height limits, tight turns and parked cars around you.

Let us know where the car is parked, whether it's in a covered area, and whether it can roll if pushed. If it's blocking a lane or a driveway, say so and we'll prioritise getting it clear.

## How we tow a broken down car

Every breakdown tow is on a tilt tray. The car rides fully on the tray, so there's no wear on the tyres, gearbox or driveline, and no kilometres added. That matters for automatics, AWDs and EVs that shouldn't be towed with wheels on the ground.

If the car rolls, it's guided on. If it won't, it's winched up gently. Once secured, we take it wherever you need it.

## What to tell us when you call

- Your location: road, direction and nearest exit, or car park and level
- Make and model, and whether it's an auto, manual, AWD or EV
- What happened: won't start, overheated, flat tyre, warning lights
- Whether the car rolls and steers
- Where you want it taken
