## Towing in Southern River: typical jobs

[Ranford Road](/roads/ranford-road) carries the bulk of Southern River's traffic, connecting the estates to Canning Vale and Armadale Road. Its intersections with Warton Road, Southern River Road and the shopping centre entrances see regular crashes, and [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) from these spots is a common job for us here.

The newer estates bring the usual flat batteries and failed starters from cars parked on the street, along with the confusion of brand new roads that some GPS apps don't know yet. In the older, semi-rural part of the suburb, we're more likely to be dealing with a horse float car that won't start, a ute stuck on a sandy driveway or an old vehicle being moved off a property.

## Roads we cover around Southern River

Ranford Road runs along the southern side of Southern River, with Harrisdale across the road and [Canning Vale](/areas/canning-vale) to the west. Warton Road leads north towards [Huntingdale](/areas/huntingdale) and Thornlie, while Southern River Road heads north-east into [Gosnells](/areas/gosnells). Holmes Street is one of the main local roads through the middle of the suburb. Tonkin Highway is a short drive east, where Ranford Road meets it near [Champion Lakes](/areas/champion-lakes).
