## Why Maida Vale drivers call us

Maida Vale sits where the coastal plain starts climbing into the hills, and that mix shapes most of our work here. Kalamunda Road is the main way up to the scarp from the airport side, so we pick up cars that have overheated on the climb, utes with blown tyres and commuters with a flat battery at the Six Ways lights. Any crash at that intersection is an [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) job where we can deal with your insurer directly.

The other half of the suburb is quieter: bush blocks, hobby farms and properties on sloping ground. After winter rain, a trailer or ute can slide off a soft gravel driveway, and that calls for a careful winch rather than a quick hook-up.

## Getting around Maida Vale

Roe Highway runs along the western edge, with Kalamunda Road crossing it and heading east through the suburb. Over Roe you're in [High Wycombe](/areas/high-wycombe) and close to the airport. At Six Ways, Hawtin Road heads south towards [Forrestfield](/areas/forrestfield), while Gooseberry Hill Road climbs into [Gooseberry Hill](/areas/gooseberry-hill). Maida Vale Road and Ridge Hill Road carry local traffic through the residential streets and up the slope.

Further up, Kalamunda Road keeps climbing to [Kalamunda](/areas/kalamunda) and the hills suburbs beyond. If your car needs to go to a workshop down on the flat, we can [transport it](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) to any mechanic you choose.

## Broken down in Maida Vale? Do this first

1. On Kalamunda Road, pull onto the verge only where it looks firm, since verges can turn soft after rain.
2. Near Six Ways, give us the exact road you're on, because the intersection has several arms.
3. On a rural block, tell us about gates, cattle grids or low branches over the driveway.
4. If your car is towing a trailer or float, mention it so we bring the right setup.
