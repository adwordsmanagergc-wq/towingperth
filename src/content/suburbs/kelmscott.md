## What brings us to Kelmscott

Kelmscott is a large, varied suburb. On the flat western side, Albany Highway is lined with shops, the Stargate Kelmscott and Kelmscott Village shopping areas, the train station and a light industrial area. That's where most of our car park and commuter callouts happen, with flat batteries, overheating engines and the odd fender-bender.

East of the river, the land rises into the Darling Scarp, with steep streets and driveways that can be tricky for older cars and heavy vehicles. A car that can't get back up a driveway, or one that's slid off a wet verge, may need a winch rather than a simple tow.

Albany Highway carries a lot of through traffic and sees regular [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) callouts, especially at busy intersections.

## Roads and neighbours around Kelmscott

[Albany Highway](/roads/albany-highway) runs north-west to [Gosnells](/areas/gosnells) and south-east to [Armadale](/areas/armadale), dividing the suburb in two. Brookton Highway branches off it, climbing east into the hills. Tonkin Highway meets Albany Highway near Kelmscott's north-western corner, while Champion Drive and Westfield Road head west into [Champion Lakes](/areas/champion-lakes) and [Camillo](/areas/camillo). Martin lies to the north, on the hills side.

## Car trouble in Kelmscott? Here's what to do

1. On Albany Highway, turn into a side street or car park if the car will move.
2. On a steep driveway, chock the wheels and don't try to roll it back down.
3. At the station or shops, note the nearest entrance or store.
4. If you're on Brookton Highway, avoid stopping on a bend.
