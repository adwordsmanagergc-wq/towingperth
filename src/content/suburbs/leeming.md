## Callouts we see most in Leeming

Leeming is a big, settled family suburb, and most of the homes have two or three cars in the driveway. That means plenty of routine [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth). Typical calls include a car that won't start before the school run, a ute with a failed fuel pump, or a teenager's first car that has finally given up. We're happy to take these to whichever workshop you prefer.

The roads around the edge produce the more urgent work. The Kwinana Freeway runs down the western side, and Roe Highway curves around the south and south-east before joining it. Merging traffic at that interchange and on Karel Avenue near the airport turnoff leads to rear-end crashes, especially in the afternoon peak. When a crash is involved, we can deal with your insurer directly.

Weekend golfers and sports teams add a few jobs too, mostly flat batteries in the car parks at Melville Glades and the local reserves.

## The road network around Leeming

South Street marks the northern boundary, with [Bull Creek](/areas/bull-creek) and Bateman across the road. Farrington Road runs east to west through the suburb towards [Murdoch](/areas/murdoch), and Karel Avenue cuts north to south, crossing Roe Highway on its way to Jandakot Airport. Findlay Road meets Farrington Road at the Shopping Forum, which is a handy reference point. To the south, across Roe Highway, is [Jandakot](/areas/jandakot), and Canning Vale lies to the east.
