## The work we do in Cockburn Central

Cockburn Central is where the Kwinana Freeway meets two of the south's busiest east-west roads, Armadale Road and Beeliar Drive, so it sees a lot of traffic and a lot of incidents. Rear-end crashes at the interchange, breakdowns on the freeway ramps and cars that stall at the lights on North Lake Road are common jobs. When a car is damaged, we handle the [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) and deal with your insurer directly.

The town centre adds a different kind of work. Apartment basements, the station car parks, Cockburn ARC and the Cockburn Gateway shopping centre next door all produce flat batteries, punctures and cars that won't start. The older light industrial streets in the suburb add the occasional work van or ute that needs taking to a workshop.

## Roads and neighbours around Cockburn Central

The Kwinana Freeway runs along the eastern edge, with [Jandakot](/areas/jandakot) and [Atwell](/areas/atwell) across it. Beeliar Drive heads west past Hammond Road, with [Success](/areas/success) along its southern side and [Yangebup](/areas/yangebup) further west, while North Lake Road runs north towards [South Lake](/areas/south-lake) and Bibra Lake. Armadale Road continues east from the freeway towards Jandakot and beyond. Midgegooroo Avenue carries local traffic through the town centre.

## Stuck in Cockburn Central? Here's what to do

1. On the freeway or its ramps, get as far left as you can and step behind the barrier.
2. In the town centre, give us the building name or the car park level and bay.
3. At the shopping centre, tell us the entrance or major store nearest your car.
4. If you're on Beeliar Drive or North Lake Road, use a side road or car park entrance if the car still moves.
