## Why Melville drivers call us

Most Melville jobs start on the highways. [Canning Highway](/roads/canning-highway) runs along the northern edge, carrying commuters between Fremantle and the city, while Leach Highway along the south is one of the main freight routes to the port. Both are busy all day, and intersections like Stock Road and Leach Highway see their share of crashes. Leach Highway breakdowns are particularly risky because of the trucks, so we make getting your car off the road the first priority.

Inside the suburb, it's quieter. Melville is mostly established homes on suburban blocks, and the calls are the familiar ones: cars that won't start after a few days parked, overheating in summer, and family cars that need towing to a mechanic. School traffic around Melville Senior High School adds a burst of activity morning and afternoon.

When you've been in a bump, we can tow the car to your repairer and handle the [insurance side](/insurance-towing) directly with your insurer.

## Roads and neighbours around Melville

Stock Road forms the western boundary, with [Palmyra](/areas/palmyra) on the far side. Canning Highway marks the north, with Attadale and Bicton beyond it. To the east is [Myaree](/areas/myaree), with its light industrial streets and plenty of mechanics, and [Alfred Cove](/areas/alfred-cove) to the north-east. Marmion Street runs east to west through the suburb, linking Stock Road with the Myaree side, and Willagee sits to the south across Leach Highway.
