## Redcliffe jobs we handle most

A lot of our Redcliffe work is tied to the airport. Drivers heading to Terminals 3 and 4 come through on Great Eastern Highway, Tonkin Highway and the local streets, and a car that gives up on the way creates a real time squeeze. We can take care of the tow while you get to your flight. Taxi, rideshare and hire car traffic also adds to the mix, along with work vans servicing the airport's commercial areas.

The two highways bring the rest. Tonkin Highway runs along the western side and Great Eastern Highway along the north, both carrying heavy freight and fast traffic. Rear-end crashes and breakdowns on these roads are classic [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) and [breakdown](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) jobs, and we'll bill your insurer directly where we can.

## Roads in and around Redcliffe

[Great Eastern Highway](/roads/great-eastern-highway) forms the northern edge, with [Ascot](/areas/ascot) and its racecourse on the other side. Tonkin Highway marks the western boundary, and Epsom Avenue links across to [Belmont](/areas/belmont). Through the middle, Fauntleroy Avenue and Dunreath Drive link the highway to the airport area, while Stanton Road serves the streets around the station.

To the south is [Cloverdale](/areas/cloverdale), and further south-east the industrial area of [Kewdale](/areas/kewdale). Redcliffe's mix of older homes, newer apartments and semi-industrial lots means some pickups are quick kerbside jobs and others need a bit of planning around gates and tight yards.

## Broken down in Redcliffe? Quick checklist

1. If you're near the airport, tell us which terminal road or car park you're closest to.
2. On Tonkin Highway, get behind the barrier if you can and stay well clear of the traffic.
3. If you have a flight to catch, say so first so we can plan the handover.
4. Note the nearest cross street if you're in the residential area around the station.
