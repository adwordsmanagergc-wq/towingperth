## What a Brookdale callout usually looks like

Brookdale is mostly residential, gazetted in the late 1990s out of the old Wungong district, with streets that fill up with school traffic morning and afternoon. A lot of our work here is ordinary [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth): a car that won't start in the driveway, a failed water pump on the school run, or a second car that has sat unused for months and needs to go to a workshop.

The edges of Brookdale are busier. Armadale Road carries heavy traffic between Armadale and the fast-growing estates further west, and it's a regular spot for rear-end crashes at intersections and turning lanes. South Western Highway runs down the eastern side towards Byford, and trucks and caravans use it as a route south. A breakdown on either road needs moving quickly.

## Brookdale roads and nearby suburbs

Armadale Road links Brookdale north-east into [Armadale](/areas/armadale) and west towards Forrestdale and the Kwinana Freeway. Forrest Road and Ninth Road run through the residential streets, and Wungong Road heads south towards the Wungong area and [Byford](/areas/byford). On the other side of South Western Highway, the land rises into Mount Richon and then [Bedfordale](/areas/bedfordale) in the hills.
