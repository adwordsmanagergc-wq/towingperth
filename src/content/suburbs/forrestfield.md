## Forrestfield jobs: what's most common

Forrestfield is a large, mostly residential suburb with the airport on its northern doorstep. Roe Highway runs past its western side, and breakdowns or crashes on it need quick action because of the fast, heavy traffic. These are standard [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs, and we'll usually bill your insurer directly.

The two shopping centres around Dawson Avenue and Strelitzia Avenue are a common pickup spot for flat batteries and cars that won't start. Hartfield Park, with its sports grounds and recreation centre, brings weekend callouts too.

Up towards the hills on the eastern side of the suburb, steeper streets and driveways can make loading a car trickier, especially for low or heavy vehicles.

## Getting around Forrestfield

Berkshire Road and Hale Road are the main arterial roads, giving access to Roe Highway and Tonkin Highway. Dawson Avenue runs through the shopping area. [High Wycombe](/areas/high-wycombe) and [Maida Vale](/areas/maida-vale) lie to the north and north-east. [Wattle Grove](/areas/wattle-grove) is to the south, and [Kewdale](/areas/kewdale) and its industrial area are a short drive west.

With freight yards and the airport so close, we also do plenty of [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) for work utes and vans travelling through.
