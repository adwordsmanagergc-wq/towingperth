## Towing jobs in Queens Park

Queens Park has changed a lot. Older houses on big blocks have given way to unit developments, especially near Welshpool Road, and plenty of residents are students and young workers driving budget cars. That makes flat batteries, overheating and worn-out clutches the bread and butter of our [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) here, often from a shared driveway or a cramped visitor bay.

The other side of Queens Park is freight. Welshpool Road runs straight into the industrial area to the north, and trucks, vans and utes from those yards use the local roads all day. Breakdowns and minor crashes along Welshpool Road and Wharf Street are common, and we also run [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) for businesses moving cars between sites.

## Roads and places around Queens Park

Welshpool Road is the main road through Queens Park, connecting west to Albany Highway at St James and east into [Welshpool](/areas/welshpool). Wharf Street and Gibbs Street carry local traffic south towards [Cannington](/areas/cannington), while Hamilton Street meets Welshpool Road at the Kewdale Road lights. Railway Parade follows the train line past Queens Park station. [East Cannington](/areas/east-cannington) lies to the east and Bentley to the west.

The Sam Kerr Football Centre and the Queens Park Reserve bushland bring weekend crowds, and St Norbert College adds school traffic on weekday mornings and afternoons.
