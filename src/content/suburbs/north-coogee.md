## What we usually handle in North Coogee

North Coogee has been transformed from old industrial land into a coastal suburb of apartments, townhouses and the Port Coogee marina. That shapes our callouts. Basement and podium car parks are common, and getting a car out of a tight underground space takes the right truck and some care. We do a fair few of those jobs as planned [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) when someone's moving or selling a car that doesn't run.

The beach and marina bring their own share. On a warm weekend, the car parks near C.Y. O'Connor Beach and Port Coogee fill up, and cars left with doors open or accessories running come back with a flat battery. Boat owners towing heavy trailers sometimes overheat their cars or burn out a clutch.

Cockburn Road along the eastern edge carries steady traffic between Fremantle and the industrial areas to the south. Crashes there are usually rear-end or turning collisions, which we tow and can bill to your insurer.

## Roads and spots around North Coogee

Cockburn Road forms the eastern boundary, with [Hamilton Hill](/areas/hamilton-hill) and Spearwood on the other side. Orsino Boulevard and Pantheon Avenue are the main streets into the Port Coogee area. To the north, the suburb runs into [South Fremantle](/areas/south-fremantle), and to the south Powell Road marks the edge with [Coogee](/areas/coogee). The old Robb Jetty site and the C.Y. O'Connor statue in the water are handy landmarks if you're near the northern beaches.
