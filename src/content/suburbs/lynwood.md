## Lynwood towing: the usual jobs

Lynwood is almost all houses, built mostly in the 1960s and 1970s, with a neighbourhood shopping centre and parks running along Bannister Creek. A lot of the cars here are second or third family cars that sit for a while, so flat batteries, seized brakes and fuel pump failures make up most of our callouts. These are straightforward [breakdown tows](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) to home or a workshop.

The edges of the suburb bring the bigger jobs. Nicholson Road and High Road both carry steady commuter traffic, and the intersections where they meet Metcalfe Road and each other see regular nose-to-tail crashes in peak hour. We also collect cars from the Riverton Forum car park just across High Road, where a car that won't start after the shopping is a familiar call.

## Roads and streets we cover in and around Lynwood

High Road wraps around the south and south-west of Lynwood and leads to Riverton Forum and [Parkwood](/areas/parkwood). [Nicholson Road](/roads/nicholson-road) runs along the eastern side, linking north to Cannington and south past Roe Highway to Thornlie and Canning Vale. Metcalfe Road forms the northern edge, with [Ferndale](/areas/ferndale) on the other side, and Lynwood Avenue carries local traffic through the middle. Across Nicholson Road lies [Langford](/areas/langford).

## Broken down in Lynwood? A few quick tips

1. If you're on Nicholson Road, turn into the nearest Lynwood side street before you stop, if you can.
2. Give us the street number, or the nearest park if you're beside Bannister Creek.
3. Let us know if the car is locked in a garage or behind a gate.
4. Tell us where the car should go, such as your own mechanic or home.
