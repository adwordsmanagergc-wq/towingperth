## Common towing jobs in Success

Success saw little housing until the Kwinana Freeway was extended in the early 1990s, and it has since become a large residential suburb with one of the southern suburbs' biggest shopping centres. Cockburn Gateway draws traffic from right across the Cockburn area, and its car parks are a regular source of calls for flat batteries, failed alternators and keys locked inside.

Being right beside the freeway brings a different type of job. Peak-hour traffic between Beeliar Drive and Russell Road can bunch up quickly, and nose-to-tail crashes are common. We tow those cars as [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) and can bill your insurer directly.

Two train stations nearby also mean plenty of cars parked all day. Commuters returning to Aubin Grove station or Cockburn Central with a car that won't start is a familiar evening call.

## Roads and places we cover around Success

The Kwinana Freeway runs along the eastern side of the suburb. Beeliar Drive crosses the north, linking [Cockburn Central](/areas/cockburn-central) with [Yangebup](/areas/yangebup) and Beeliar, while Russell Road crosses the south near Aubin Grove station. Hammond Road runs north to south through the suburb and leads down to [Hammond Park](/areas/hammond-park).

Wentworth Parade is one of the main local streets and passes close to Cockburn Gateway. Across the freeway, Atwell and Aubin Grove are a short drive away.
