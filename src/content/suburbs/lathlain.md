## Getting around Lathlain

Lathlain is a small, hilly suburb. The [Great Eastern Highway](/roads/great-eastern-highway) runs past its north-western edge towards Burswood, while Orrong Road divides it from [Rivervale](/areas/rivervale) and leads south-east towards [Kewdale](/areas/kewdale). Roberts Road marks the south-eastern side, and Rutland Avenue runs beside the railway line on the way to [Victoria Park](/areas/victoria-park), where the nearest train station sits.

Lathlain Park, home of the West Coast Eagles training base and the Perth Football Club, is the suburb's biggest landmark. On busy days the surrounding streets fill quickly, which affects where a truck can stop.
