## What North Lake callouts tend to be

North Lake is mostly bushland, wetland and quiet residential streets, so our work here is a mix of road calls and everyday breakdowns. Farrington Road is the busiest local road. It runs along the northern edge between the freeway and Kardinya, past the lake and Murdoch University, and it carries traffic to the hospitals and campus at Murdoch. Peak-hour crashes and breakdowns on Farrington Road are common callouts.

Sports traffic is the other regular source. Lakeside Recreation Centre on the corner of Farrington Road and Bibra Drive draws big crowds for basketball, and cars left in the car park on a hot afternoon often come back with a flat battery. We handle these as straightforward [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth), taking the car home or to your mechanic.

The residential streets are calm, and most calls there are from families whose second car has finally stopped for good.

## Roads and neighbours around North Lake

Farrington Road forms the northern boundary, with [Murdoch](/areas/murdoch) across the road. The Kwinana Freeway runs along the east, and [Leeming](/areas/leeming) sits on the other side. To the south, the old Roe Highway road reserve separates the suburb from [Bibra Lake](/areas/bibra-lake), and North Lake Road marks the western edge with Coolbellup and Kardinya. Bibra Drive and Progress Drive are the main internal roads, running past the lake and the reserve.
