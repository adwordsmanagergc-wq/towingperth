## What we tow in Samson

Most of Samson is a compact, settled residential area of brick and tile homes, so the callouts are mostly everyday ones. Cars that won't start in the driveway, a family car with a failed clutch, or a vehicle that has been sitting unused and needs to go to a mechanic. For those we offer straightforward [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) to whichever workshop you prefer.

The roads around the edge are where the more urgent calls come from. Stock Road is part of National Route 1 and carries a lot of freight, and South Street is a main east to west link between Fremantle and Murdoch. Rear-end crashes at the lights and breakdowns in peak traffic are the typical jobs, and we can deal directly with your insurer.

Samson Park and the recreation centre opposite also bring visitors in for sport and dog walks, and their car parks see the occasional flat battery.

## Roads and places we cover around Samson

South Street runs along the northern edge, heading west into [Hilton](/areas/hilton) and east through [Kardinya](/areas/kardinya) towards Murdoch and Fiona Stanley Hospital. Stock Road forms the western boundary with [Hamilton Hill](/areas/hamilton-hill) and continues south to Spearwood and Bibra Lake. Winterfold Road closes off the southern side next to Coolbellup.

Inside the suburb, McCombe Avenue carries the local bus route, and short crescents and cul-de-sacs branch off the surrounding streets. Those dead-end streets can be tight for a tilt tray, so a heads-up on parked cars helps.

1. On Stock Road, pull as far onto the verge as possible and stay clear of passing trucks.
2. In a cul-de-sac, tell us if the car is blocking a driveway or if the street is narrow.

4. Have your rego and where you want the car to go ready when you call.
