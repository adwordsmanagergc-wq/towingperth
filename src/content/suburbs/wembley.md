## How Wembley breakdowns usually happen

Wembley is mostly family homes, but it's crossed by some of the busiest roads in the inner west. Cambridge Street, Grantham Street, Selby Street and Harborne Street all carry heavy traffic at peak times, and rear-end crashes at their lights are a regular source of work. Those are [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs, and we can bill your insurer directly.

The other half of our Wembley work is quieter. A car that won't start in the driveway, a family wagon with a dead battery at the shops, or a car that needs [transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) to a mechanic for a job that can't be fixed at the roadside.

## Getting around Wembley

Cambridge Street runs east to west through the shopping strip, linking [West Leederville](/areas/west-leederville) with [Floreat](/areas/floreat). Grantham Street runs parallel to the north, and Salvado Road follows the southern edge towards [Jolimont](/areas/jolimont) and Subiaco. Selby Street and Harborne Street carry north-south traffic, and Herdsman Parade curves around the southern shore of Herdsman Lake. Lake Monger sits on the eastern side near the freeway.

The housing is a mix of older homes on wide blocks and newer townhouses with tight driveways. If the car is in a narrow garage or a rear unit, describe the access so we bring the right truck.
