## What we see most in Beechboro

Reid Highway runs along the northern side of Beechboro, carrying traffic between the northern suburbs and the Swan Valley. It's a high-speed road, and a car that loses power or blows a tyre here needs to be moved quickly. We handle these as [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) jobs and get the car off the highway to wherever you want it.

Inside the suburb, Beechboro is mostly family homes on curving streets. Callouts here are often a car that won't start in the morning, a dead battery after a weekend away, or a car that's been sitting on the verge and finally needs to go to a wrecker or a workshop.

Crashes also happen at the busy intersections along Beechboro Road North and Altone Road, especially around school times.

## Main roads around Beechboro

Beechboro Road North and Altone Road both connect the suburb to Reid Highway, and Beechboro Road North also carries traffic south towards [Morley](/areas/morley). Benara Road passes Altone Park and links across to [Kiara](/areas/kiara) and [Lockridge](/areas/lockridge). North of Reid Highway, [Malaga](/areas/malaga) has a big industrial area where many people take their cars for repairs.

We know the area well enough to find the quiet cul-de-sacs, but a street number and a nearby landmark always help.

## What to do if you're stuck in Beechboro

1. On Reid Highway, stay well off the road and don't stand near passing traffic.
2. In a cul-de-sac, mention if the car is blocked in by others, so we can plan the pickup.
3. If you've had a crash, take photos of both cars and the scene before anything is moved.
4. Let us know the destination, whether that's home, a mechanic in Malaga or your insurer's repairer.
