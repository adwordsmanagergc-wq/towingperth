## What we're usually called out for in Jindalee

Jindalee is still growing, and that shows in our callouts. Builders' vehicles get stuck on soft sand at house sites, and tradies' utes break down mid-job. A lot of families here also commute a long way south each day, so cars that do big kilometres sometimes fail at the worst moment, in the driveway at 6 am or halfway home on Marmion Avenue.

Because the suburb is right on the coast, we also see beach-day problems. Cars left in the sun all afternoon with a phone charger plugged in can come back to a flat battery, and salt air is hard on older vehicles. For anything that won't go, our [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) service gets the car to your mechanic or home.

## Roads and places we cover around Jindalee

Marmion Avenue forms the eastern boundary, separating Jindalee from [Butler](/areas/butler). Hampshire Drive marks the southern edge, with [Quinns Rocks](/areas/quinns-rocks) beyond it. To the north, the estate gives way to [Alkimos](/areas/alkimos) near Eglinton Rocks, and the Indian Ocean lines the west.

Inside the suburb, Jindalee Boulevard and Santa Barbara Parade carry most of the local traffic between homes, parks and the coast. Some streets here are fairly recent, so a nearby landmark as well as an address helps the driver find you.
