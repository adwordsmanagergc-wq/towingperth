## Beeliar's most common tow jobs

Most of Beeliar is housing estates built since the 1990s, laid out around parks and the edge of the Beeliar Regional Park wetlands. Inside the suburb, our jobs are the everyday kind: cars that won't start in the driveway, flat tyres, failed alternators and cars that need [transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) to a mechanic in Bibra Lake or Cockburn Central.

The busier calls come from the roads on the edges. Beeliar Drive carries traffic between the coast at Coogee and the Kwinana Freeway at Cockburn Central, and its busy signalised junctions see plenty of minor crashes. Russell Road on the southern side is used by trucks heading for the industrial areas, so breakdowns there need careful handling. For a crash on either road, we arrange the [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) and can bill your insurer.

## Roads and neighbours around Beeliar

Beeliar Drive forms the boundary with [Yangebup](/areas/yangebup) to the north, and continues east past Hammond Road to [Cockburn Central](/areas/cockburn-central) and the freeway. Russell Road marks the southern boundary with [Wattleup](/areas/wattleup). To the west is [Munster](/areas/munster), and to the east is [Success](/areas/success), with Thomsons Lake Nature Reserve taking up a large share of the suburb's land.

Spearwood Avenue and Durnin Avenue carry local traffic through the estates and towards the shops.

## Broken down in Beeliar? Here's what helps

1. On Beeliar Drive, use a side street or the shopping centre if you can roll in safely.
2. Keep clear of the wetland verges; the ground can be soft after rain.
3. Tell us the street, house number or nearest park so we can find you fast.
4. If you've been in a crash, swap details and take photos before the car moves.
