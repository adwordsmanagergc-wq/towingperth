## Why Marangaroo drivers call us

Marangaroo is a residential suburb, and the jobs here mostly start in driveways and on local streets. Older cars that have done a lot of kilometres fail at awkward times, often in the morning when the starter won't engage or overnight when a battery has drained. We load them up and take them to a mechanic, often in nearby Wangara or Malaga.

Its four boundary roads are where most of the serious towing happens. Wanneroo Road carries a mix of trucks and commuters, Mirrabooka Avenue is a major north-south link, and both Hepburn Avenue and Marangaroo Drive have busy signalised intersections. Rear-end crashes and side impacts at those lights are typical [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs, and we'll bill your insurer where we can.

## Roads and places we cover around Marangaroo

[Wanneroo Road](/roads/wanneroo-road) forms the western boundary, facing Greenwood and Warwick. Hepburn Avenue marks the north, with [Madeley](/areas/madeley) and [Landsdale](/areas/landsdale) across the road, while Mirrabooka Avenue on the east separates Marangaroo from [Alexander Heights](/areas/alexander-heights). To the south, Marangaroo Drive borders [Girrawheen](/areas/girrawheen).

Inside the suburb, Aylesford Drive leads to the golf course, and a network of quiet residential streets connects to all four main roads. We know the shortcuts through, which matters when one of the boundary roads is jammed after a crash.
