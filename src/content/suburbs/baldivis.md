## What we're called out for in Baldivis

Baldivis is one of Perth's big growth suburbs, and new estates bring new kinds of trouble. Roadworks, fresh roundabouts and building sites mean punctures, kerbed wheels and minor collisions. Families with two or three cars mean plenty of flat batteries and failed starters in driveways, and the Stockland Baldivis car park on Safety Bay Road is a regular pickup.

The Kwinana Freeway is the other half of our Baldivis work. Long commutes north to Perth wear cars out, and overheating, tyre blowouts and gearbox failures on the freeway are common. Crashes near the Safety Bay Road and Mundijong Road interchanges often become [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs where we bill your insurer directly.

Out east, rural Baldivis brings utes, horse floats and 4WDs stuck in soft sand, which is where our [4WD recovery](/services/4wd-recovery-perth) gear comes in.

## Getting to you around Baldivis

Safety Bay Road is the main east-west link, heading out to [Warnbro](/areas/warnbro) and [Rockingham](/areas/rockingham). Baldivis Road runs north-south through the middle, splitting the estates from the rural side, and there's more on it in our [Baldivis Road guide](/roads/baldivis-road). Nairn Drive loops through the newer estates, while Mundijong Road meets the freeway in the north, where [Kulija Road](/roads/kulija-road) continues west. [Wellard](/areas/wellard) sits just to the north and [Port Kennedy](/areas/port-kennedy) to the south-west.
