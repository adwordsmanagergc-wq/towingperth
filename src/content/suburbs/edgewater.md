## Towing in Edgewater: what we see most

With Lake Joondalup on its eastern edge and almost no through traffic, Edgewater gives us mostly ordinary household jobs. Cars that won't start after a long weekend, worn-out batteries and vehicles that need a lift to a workshop are typical. Families at Mater Dei College and the local primary school add school-run breakdowns on Treetop Avenue and the streets around it.

Edgewater station is the other regular pickup spot. Commuters park there from early morning, and a dead battery at the end of the day is a common reason people call us. Beside the station, the [Mitchell Freeway](/roads/mitchell-freeway) brings fast-moving traffic past the suburb, and freeway breakdowns and crashes nearby often become [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs that we bill straight to the insurer.

## Roads and neighbouring suburbs

[Joondalup Drive](/roads/joondalup-drive) forms Edgewater's western boundary and starts at a T-junction with Ocean Reef Road, which runs along the south. Across Joondalup Drive and the freeway is [Heathridge](/areas/heathridge), and to the north the suburb flows into the [Joondalup](/areas/joondalup) city centre. South across Ocean Reef Road is [Woodvale](/areas/woodvale). Eddystone Avenue meets Joondalup Drive on the way north, and Treetop Avenue runs through the middle of the suburb.
