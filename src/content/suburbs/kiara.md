## Common towing jobs in Kiara

Kiara is mostly family homes on curving streets, with two schools in the suburb and the shops of Morley just to the west. Our callouts here are usually at people's homes: a car that won't start before the school run, a battery that has died after a few days parked, or a car that needs to go to a mechanic for a gearbox or cooling problem. We also tow plenty of older second cars and P-plater runabouts that have finally given up.

The roads around the suburb carry a lot more traffic than the streets inside it. Benara Road, Altone Road and Morley Drive East are all major routes for people heading between Morley, Bassendean, Beechboro and the Swan Valley, and the larger intersections see rear-end and turning crashes in peak hour. Those jobs are [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth), and we can bill your insurer directly so you don't have to pay first.

## Roads and places we cover around Kiara

Benara Road and Morley Drive East carry the east-west traffic past the suburb, and Altone Road links the two north to south. Inside Kiara, Wheatstone Drive and Bottlebrush Drive carry most of the local traffic past the residential pockets and parks.

To the east is [Lockridge](/areas/lockridge), to the north [Beechboro](/areas/beechboro), and to the south [Eden Hill](/areas/eden-hill). [Morley](/areas/morley), with its big shopping centre, sits right next door to the west. If you need a car moved without a breakdown involved, for example after buying it privately, use our [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) service.
