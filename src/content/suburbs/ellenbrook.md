## Towing in Ellenbrook: common situations

Ellenbrook is a large and still-growing suburb with a busy town centre, plenty of young families and lots of commuters. Many of our jobs start in car parks at Ellenbrook Central and around the train station on the Ellenbrook line, where flat batteries and cars that won't start are the most common calls.

Drumpellier Drive and The Promenade carry heavy traffic in peak hour, and crashes at roundabouts and intersections are regular [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) work for us. In the newer estates, roadworks, unsealed verges and changing street layouts can make finding a car harder, so good directions matter.

## Roads and connections around Ellenbrook

Drumpellier Drive links Ellenbrook south to Reid Highway, passing Gnangara Road on the way. Heading west, The Promenade connects to Tonkin Highway, while heading east it leads into the town centre. The Parkway and Main Street carry most of the local traffic through the centre, near Ellenbrook Central and the station.

Whiteman Park sits to the south-west. Further south, the roads lead to [Ballajura](/areas/ballajura), [Malaga](/areas/malaga) and [Beechboro](/areas/beechboro), while the Swan Valley and [Herne Hill](/areas/herne-hill) are to the south-east.

## What to do if you're stuck in Ellenbrook

1. In a car park, note the nearest store, row or station entrance.
2. In a new estate, share a map pin from your phone along with the closest street sign you can see.
3. On Drumpellier Drive or The Promenade, pull over well clear of roundabouts.
4. If you've had a crash, take photos and swap details before the car is moved.
