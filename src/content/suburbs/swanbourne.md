## Why Swanbourne drivers call us

Swanbourne is a quiet coastal suburb, but it has a busy edge. On summer days, the car park at Swanbourne Beach fills up, and cars that sat in the sun with the stereo running or the doors open often need a jump or a tow by evening. We also get calls for cars that won't start in the narrow streets of older homes near the station, which are ordinary [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) jobs.

West Coast Highway begins here and heads north past Campbell Barracks and the rifle range towards City Beach. It's a fast road with long stretches of bushland either side, so a breakdown at night can feel isolated. We'll come to you and get the car moved.

## Swanbourne's roads and neighbours

Curtin Avenue comes up from [Cottesloe](/areas/cottesloe) and becomes West Coast Highway, which runs north to [City Beach](/areas/city-beach). Marine Parade leads to the beach, and North Street runs along the southern side towards the railway. Stirling Road marks the eastern edge near Lake Claremont, and [Claremont](/areas/claremont) and [Mount Claremont](/areas/mount-claremont) sit to the east and north-east.

Servetus Street and the streets around Allen Park carry local traffic, with plenty of families and sports traffic on weekends.

## If you're stranded in Swanbourne

1. At the beach, tell us which part of the car park you're in and whether you're near the surf club or the path.
2. On West Coast Highway, keep away from the traffic side and give us the nearest landmark.
3. In the residential streets, a house number and cross street help us find you fast.
4. Tell us where the car needs to go, and whether you need a lift arranged separately.
