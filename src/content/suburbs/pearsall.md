## The usual jobs in Pearsall

Most callouts in Pearsall are everyday family car problems. Flat batteries on cold mornings, cars that won't start after school pickup at Pearsall Primary, and the occasional car that's been sitting on the verge and needs to go to a mechanic. Those are straightforward [breakdown tows](/services/breakdown-towing-perth), and because Wangara is next door, the trip to a workshop is short.

The busier work happens on the edges. [Wanneroo Road](/roads/wanneroo-road) runs along the western side of Pearsall, and the grade-separated interchange with Ocean Reef Road funnels a lot of traffic past the suburb. Lane changes near the ramps and sudden stops in the queues are behind many of the crashes we attend, and we can usually deal with your insurer directly.

## Finding your way around Pearsall

Willespie Drive is the main loop through Pearsall, past the primary school, parks and the local shopping centre. Kemp Street and the streets off it carry traffic north towards [Hocking](/areas/hocking) and the older parts of [Wanneroo](/areas/wanneroo). Ocean Reef Road crosses the area on its way east to [Wangara](/areas/wangara) and Landsdale, and west across Wanneroo Road towards Woodvale and the freeway.

Lake Goollelal and the Yellagonga wetlands lie just across Wanneroo Road. Those western roads are tree-lined and dark at night, so clear directions matter if you break down there after sunset.

## What to do if your car stops in Pearsall

1. In a residential street, park as close to the kerb as you can and leave room for the truck to pull in behind or in front.
2. On Wanneroo Road or Ocean Reef Road, put the hazards on and stand well clear of the traffic.
3. Tell us if the car has a flat tyre, a locked steering wheel or no keys, so we bring the right equipment.
4. Have your destination ready, whether that's home, a Wangara workshop or a smash repairer.
