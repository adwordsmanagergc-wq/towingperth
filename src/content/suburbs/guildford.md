## Towing in Guildford: the usual jobs

Even with the bypass further south, [Great Eastern Highway](/roads/great-eastern-highway) still winds right through the old town, coming over the Helena River as Johnson Street, turning along James Street past the shops and heading north on East Street across the railway. That means heavy traffic, tight corners and parked cars in the same space, and we regularly clear minor crashes and breakdowns from these streets. Where another car is involved, we handle it as [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) and deal with your insurer directly.

Guildford is also the gateway to the Swan Valley, with West Swan Road crossing the river and heading north towards the wineries. Weekend visitors with flat batteries, cars that won't start after a long lunch and people who have decided not to drive home all keep us busy. Then there are the old houses themselves: narrow driveways, rear lanes and heritage garages that need a careful approach.

## Roads and places we cover around Guildford

James Street, Johnson Street and Meadow Street carry most of the traffic through the centre of town, close to Stirling Square. Guildford Road comes in from the west through [Bassendean](/areas/bassendean), and West Swan Road leads out to the Swan Valley. Guildford and East Guildford stations sit on the Midland line through town.

The Swan and Helena rivers meet on Guildford's edge, and the Helena River separates the town from [South Guildford](/areas/south-guildford). To the north-west is [Eden Hill](/areas/eden-hill), and [Midland](/areas/midland) is a short run east along the highway. For classic or heritage vehicles that need moving rather than towing, our [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) service is a good fit.

## If you're stuck in Guildford right now

1. On the highway through town, use a parking bay or side street rather than stopping in a traffic lane.

3. If you're at a winery or cafe, tell us the business name and which car park you're in.
4. Heritage driveways can be tight, so mention gates, low carports or rear-lane access.
