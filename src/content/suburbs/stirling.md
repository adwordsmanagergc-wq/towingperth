## Common Stirling tow jobs

Stirling is about 10 kilometres up the [Mitchell Freeway](/roads/mitchell-freeway) from the city, and the freeway runs along its western and southern sides. That stretch near the Karrinyup Road interchange is heavy with commuters morning and afternoon, which makes it a regular spot for rear-end crashes and breakdowns in the left lane. Clearing those quickly is our priority, and we'll talk to your insurer about the bill so you don't need to.

The rest of our Stirling work is close to home. Cars left all day in the Stirling station park-and-ride go flat. Visitors to Osborne Park Hospital occasionally find a car that won't start after a long shift or a long wait. In the residential streets, with their mix of older family homes and newer builds, we tow plenty of everyday [breakdowns](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) to local mechanics.

## Roads and nearby suburbs around Stirling

Cedric Street is the spine of the suburb, running from the freeway past the council offices and Stirling Village shops. Karrinyup Road meets the freeway at the main interchange, running east through the suburb towards Wanneroo Road and west to [Karrinyup](/areas/karrinyup) and the coast. Amelia Street and Poincaire Street form the northern edge with [Balcatta](/areas/balcatta), and Erindale Road heads north towards the Balcatta industrial area. To the south lies [Osborne Park](/areas/osborne-park), and [Innaloo](/areas/innaloo) is just across the freeway.

Stirling Civic Gardens and the lakes and parks around them take up a fair bit of land, so some streets are cul-de-sacs that back onto reserves.

## If your car won't go in Stirling

1. On the freeway, stay behind the barrier with your hazards on and note how far you are from Karrinyup Road.
2. In the station car park, find a sign or bay marker so the driver can go straight to you.
3. At the hospital, tell security you're waiting for a tow so the truck can be let through.
4. Tell us the destination when you call, even if it's just home.
