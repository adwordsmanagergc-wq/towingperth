## Woodlands jobs we see most

Woodlands has a big residential core, a busy commercial edge and a lake in the middle. Inside the suburb, our jobs are the everyday ones: a car that won't start in the driveway, a dead battery at the Woodlands Village shops on Rosewood Avenue, or a car that needs to go to a mechanic. Those are standard [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) jobs.

The northern edge is different. Scarborough Beach Road carries heavy traffic past shops, the cinema on Liege Street and the Innaloo shopping area across the road. Rear-end crashes and broken-down cars on that road need moving quickly, and we handle the collisions as accident recovery with insurer billing.

## Roads and neighbours around Woodlands

Scarborough Beach Road forms the northern boundary, linking [Osborne Park](/areas/osborne-park) to the east with [Innaloo](/areas/innaloo) and the coast. Pearson Street runs through the suburb and is a busy local route. Rosewood Avenue and Huntriss Road lead into the village centre and the streets around Jackadder Lake. To the south-west lies [Wembley Downs](/areas/wembley-downs), and Herdsman Lake and Wembley sit to the south-east.

Many streets in Woodlands are named after trees, and plenty of them curve, so a house number and cross street are the quickest way to help our driver find you.
