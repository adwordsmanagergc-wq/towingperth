## What we're called to in Koongamia

Koongamia was built as a State Housing Commission suburb, and it is still a compact pocket of homes on quiet streets rather than a shopping or industrial area. That makes most of our work here simple but important: cars that won't start in the driveway, older vehicles with a failed fuel pump or alternator, and cars that need to go to a nearby workshop. When someone is selling or moving a car that doesn't run, we also handle the move as [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth).

The through roads bring the rest. Clayton Street and Scott Street carry traffic between Midland, Bellevue, Helena Valley and Greenmount, and the corners where they meet the local streets see the occasional low-speed crash. If that happens to you, our [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) service can clear the car and deal with your insurer directly.

## Roads and places we cover around Koongamia

Clayton Street is the main link west towards Midland, and Scott Street runs through the suburb on its way between Helena Valley and Greenmount. Jinda Road, Banjine Road and Coulston Road carry the local traffic. Great Eastern Highway and Roe Highway are both close by for longer tows.

[Bellevue](/areas/bellevue) wraps around Koongamia on the western and northern sides, [Boya](/areas/boya) sits to the east and [Helena Valley](/areas/helena-valley) lies to the south. Greenmount Hill rises just beyond, marking the start of the climb into the Perth Hills.
