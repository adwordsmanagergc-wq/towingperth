## What gets us called to Riverton

Leach Highway runs through Riverton on its way between Kewdale and Fremantle, and it carries a heavy mix of cars and trucks. The approach to Shelley Bridge, where lanes merge before the river crossing, is a known congestion spot, and rear-end crashes there are a regular job. We handle these as [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth), with insurer billing so you're not out of pocket upfront.

Away from the highway, Riverton is a quiet, established suburb with a lot of older residents and, more and more, young families. We tow plenty of cars that have sat unused for a while, and others that need to go to a workshop after a failure on the way to the shops. The Riverton Leisureplex and Riverton Forum, just across High Road in Parkwood, are frequent pickup points.

## Roads and places we cover around Riverton

Leach Highway cuts through the suburb, crossing the river via Shelley Bridge to [Wilson](/areas/wilson). High Road meets Leach Highway at the Riverton, Shelley and Willetton corner and then runs east past the Leisureplex towards [Parkwood](/areas/parkwood) and Lynwood. Vahland Avenue heads south from Leach Highway towards Willetton, and Riley Road and Riverton Drive run through the older streets near the river. The old two-lane Riverton Bridge still carries local traffic across the Canning. [Shelley](/areas/shelley) is directly to the north-west, and Ferndale borders the east.
