## Typical Midvale tows

Most of our Midvale work comes off the two highways. Roe Highway runs through the suburb with traffic lights at Morrison Road, and that intersection sees its share of rear-end bumps when traffic banks up. We clear the damaged car and, for [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth), we can bill your insurer directly.

[Great Eastern Highway](/roads/great-eastern-highway) forms the southern boundary, and it's a common place for older cars to overheat as they start the climb towards Greenmount. Inside the suburb, the streets are mostly established housing, with a fair amount of redeveloped public housing. Here the jobs are everyday ones: a car that won't start in the driveway, a failed clutch or a car that needs a ride to a workshop in Midland.

## Roads that run through Midvale

Morrison Road is the main east to west route, linking [Midland](/areas/midland) to Swan View. Farrall Road runs north towards [Stratton](/areas/stratton), and Roe Highway cuts across the western part of the suburb. The eastern edge follows the railway line, with [Swan View](/areas/swan-view) on the other side.

South of Great Eastern Highway you're into [Bellevue](/areas/bellevue) and Koongamia, with the hills rising behind them. Part of Midvale sits on what was once the Helena Vale Racecourse, which gave the suburb half its name.
