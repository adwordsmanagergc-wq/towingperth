## Common tow jobs in Greenwood

Most Greenwood callouts come from its edges. The Mitchell Freeway runs down the western side, and morning traffic heading south towards Stirling bunches up past Hepburn Avenue and Warwick Road. When it stops suddenly, the result is often a nose-to-tail crash, and that's an [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) job where we deal with your insurer for you.

Wanneroo Road on the eastern side brings a different mix: cars overheating at the lights, gearboxes giving up in stop-start traffic, and the occasional side-swipe at a busy intersection. Inside the suburb it's quieter. We mostly collect cars that won't start in a driveway or at Greenwood Village, and take them to a mechanic.

## Roads and places we cover around Greenwood

Hepburn Avenue forms the northern edge, with [Kingsley](/areas/kingsley) on the other side, while Warwick Road marks the south and leads across to [Warwick](/areas/warwick). The freeway separates Greenwood from [Duncraig](/areas/duncraig), and over Wanneroo Road lies Marangaroo. Both the [Mitchell Freeway](/roads/mitchell-freeway) and [Wanneroo Road](/roads/wanneroo-road) are on our list of roads that see a lot of crashes.

Inside the suburb, Calectasia Street leads to the main shopping centre, and Canham Way holds the small commercial area with tyre and trade businesses. We also cover Greenwood College and the schools on the residential streets in between.

## Stuck in Greenwood? Do this first

1. On the freeway, move left onto the shoulder and stay behind the barrier if there is one.
2. On Wanneroo Road or Hepburn Avenue, try to turn into the nearest side street rather than stopping in a traffic lane.
3. Note the closest exit, shop or school so the driver can find you without guessing.
4. If another car is involved, photograph both vehicles and swap details before anything is moved.
