## Why Huntingdale drivers call us

Huntingdale is almost entirely residential, and our jobs here are mostly about family cars and daily routines. School runs, commutes and weekend sport mean a lot of short trips, and short trips are hard on batteries. Dead batteries, starter motor failures and cars that won't come out of park are typical calls. When the problem can't be fixed on the spot, it becomes a [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) job to the workshop of your choice.

The main roads carry a fair bit of through traffic. Warton Road links to Canning Vale and the industrial estates there, and Southern River Road runs south-west into the growth suburbs. Bumps at roundabouts and intersections on these roads are the other common reason we get called.

## Roads and neighbouring suburbs

Warton Road cuts across the suburb on a diagonal, heading north-east towards Maddington and south-west towards Canning Vale and Ranford Road, with [Thornlie](/areas/thornlie) just to the north-west. Southern River Road runs along the southern side, towards [Southern River](/areas/southern-river). Corfield Street runs east towards the [Gosnells](/areas/gosnells) town centre, while Huntingdale Road and Gay Street carry local traffic through the middle of the suburb.
