## Why Rossmoyne drivers call us

Rossmoyne is a settled residential area, and most of our work here comes from home driveways and the streets around the shopping centre on Central Road. Cars that have been sitting in the garage, a battery that's finally given up or an engine light that won't go away are classic [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) jobs. Families with a teenager learning to drive also call us when an older first car calls it quits on the way to school.

Leach Highway along the southern edge brings the bigger jobs. It's a major freight route between Kewdale and Fremantle, and traffic banks up towards Shelley Bridge in peak hour. Rear-end crashes along this stretch are common, and we tow those cars to a repairer and deal with your insurer.

## Roads and places around Rossmoyne

Leach Highway marks the southern boundary, with Karel Avenue and Webb Street meeting it at the Willetton and [Bull Creek](/areas/bull-creek) sides. Central Road runs east to west through the middle of Rossmoyne, past the shopping centre. Fifth Avenue follows the eastern side next to [Shelley](/areas/shelley), and Riverton Drive West serves the northern streets closer to the river. Bull Creek Road runs through the western side, the closest part of the suburb to Bull Creek and [Brentwood](/areas/brentwood).

If you need a car moved for a sale, a restoration or a long trip, our [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) service covers Rossmoyne too.
