## Beaconsfield breakdowns and crashes

Beaconsfield is a hilly, older suburb of mostly single homes, wedged between some of Fremantle's busiest roads. South Street runs along the top, carrying traffic west into Fremantle and inland towards Murdoch, while Hampton Road and Carrington Street run north to south down either side. Those three roads give us most of our work here, with rear-end crashes at the lights and older cars that give up climbing the hill. Damaged cars go out as [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth), with insurer billing where you need it.

The suburb's location close to the port and the light industrial streets of O'Connor also means some of our calls are for trade vehicles and vans, and the occasional container move through our [container transport](/services/container-transport-perth) service. Inside the suburb, we often collect cars that have been stored in a driveway or carport for a long time and need a tilt tray to a mechanic.

## The roads that frame Beaconsfield

Hampton Road forms the western edge, separating Beaconsfield from [South Fremantle](/areas/south-fremantle), and leads south into [Hamilton Hill](/areas/hamilton-hill). South Street runs along the northern side, with [White Gum Valley](/areas/white-gum-valley) and the Fremantle city centre beyond. Carrington Street runs down the eastern edge next to [Hilton](/areas/hilton), and Lefroy Road crosses through the middle of the suburb.

Many of the streets here are narrow with cars parked on the verge, so tell us about access if you're in a tight spot.

## Car trouble in Beaconsfield? Quick checklist

1. On South Street or Hampton Road, aim for a side street or a car park if the car can still move.
2. If you're on a slope, chock a wheel or turn the wheels into the kerb.
3. Tell us the cross street and which side of the road you're on.
4. Let us know if it's a van or work vehicle with a load, so we send a suitable truck.
