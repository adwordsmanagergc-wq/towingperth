## Our usual Morley callouts

The Galleria and the commercial area around it are the busiest part of Morley for us. Shoppers come back to flat batteries, failed starters or cars that won't come out of park, and the multi-level parking means we sometimes have to plan around low clearances. Walter Road West and the streets around the centre are also busy with traffic, buses and pedestrians, which leads to the odd fender bender.

Tonkin Highway is the other big source of work. Since it was built through Morley in 1984, it has carried heavy commuter and freight traffic between the northern suburbs, the airport and the south-east. Breakdowns and crashes on the highway near the Benara Road and Collier Road interchanges become [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs, and we can bill your insurer directly.

## Roads and places we cover around Morley

Tonkin Highway splits Morley in two, with Morley Drive and Walter Road crossing it from east to west. To the west is [Dianella](/areas/dianella), while to the north-east lies [Beechboro](/areas/beechboro), and [Kiara](/areas/kiara) is on the eastern side. Collier Road heads south-east from Morley towards Bassendean, and [Bayswater](/areas/bayswater) is to the south-east past Embleton.

We also cover the residential streets around Morley, as well as the Morley train station precinct.
