## Common City Beach callouts

Most jobs here begin with a day at the beach. Doors left open for hours, a stereo running while the kids swim, or a phone charging off the battery can leave a car flat by late afternoon in the City Beach and Floreat Beach car parks. Salt and sand also find their way into older cars, and we see seized brakes and stubborn starters from regular beachgoers.

The other big source of work is West Coast Highway. It carries summer traffic between Scarborough and Swanbourne, and the side-street intersections around Challenger Parade and Oceanic Drive see rear-end and turning crashes. We clear those as insured jobs, and we take the car wherever your insurer or you choose.

Behind the dunes, City Beach is quiet residential streets with large homes and plenty of European cars. Low-clearance vehicles get loaded on a tilt tray with soft straps so nothing scrapes.

## Getting around City Beach with a tow truck

Oceanic Drive runs east from the coast to [Floreat](/areas/floreat) and on towards the city, while The Boulevard links the northern end of the suburb inland past Floreat Forum. Kalinda Drive and Oceanic Drive frame much of Bold Park, the big bushland reserve on the southern side, so there's no through road there and we come in from the highway or the east.

North along the coast you reach [Scarborough](/areas/scarborough); to the south is [Swanbourne](/areas/swanbourne). For cars that just need moving rather than rescuing, such as a car heading to a buyer or a restorer, our [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) service covers that too.

3. If the car is lowered or has a front spoiler, mention it so we bring the right ramps.
4. Lock up valuables and keep your keys with you while you wait on the grass.
