## The jobs that bring us to Coolbellup

Coolbellup started life as a state housing suburb in the 1960s, and these days it's a mix of original brick cottages and newer homes on big blocks. Many households have older cars, second cars and project cars, so failed starters, overheating and long-stored vehicles that need a tilt tray are common jobs. We also move cars to mechanics and wreckers using our [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) service.

The busier calls come from the roads around the suburb's edges. Stock Road on the western side is a major freight route, and North Lake Road on the eastern side carries steady traffic between Melville and Cockburn Central. Breakdowns and crashes on either road need clearing quickly, and we handle damaged cars as [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs with insurer billing.

## Roads around Coolbellup and where they lead

Coolbellup is neatly framed by four roads. Winterfold Road runs along the north, with [Samson](/areas/samson) on the other side, and Forrest Road runs along the south, next to [Bibra Lake](/areas/bibra-lake). Stock Road on the west separates the suburb from [Hamilton Hill](/areas/hamilton-hill), and North Lake Road on the east runs past [North Lake](/areas/north-lake) and Kardinya. Inside, Coolbellup Avenue, Counsel Road and Waverley Road carry most of the local traffic.

## Car trouble in Coolbellup? Here's how to make it quick

1. On Stock Road or North Lake Road, get off the main road into a side street if you can.
2. At the Coolbellup shops, tell us whether you're in the centre's car park, beside the library or out on the street.
3. For stored cars, describe what's blocking access, like a gate, a fence or another car.
4. Have the address ready for where the car should go.
