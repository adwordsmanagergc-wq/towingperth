## What we tow out of Osborne Park

Osborne Park is a working suburb. Scarborough Beach Road holds one of the largest car yard strips in the country, and the streets around Hutton Street, Howe Street and Walters Drive are packed with showrooms, warehouses, trade suppliers and offices. That means a lot of [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth): new and used cars moved between yards, cars heading to a service department, or a trade-in that won't start.

Fleet vans and work utes also break down here mid-shift, and we tow them to the owner's mechanic so the business can get on with the day. Some businesses also need containers moved in or out, which we handle through our [container transport](/services/container-transport-perth) service.

## Roads through Osborne Park

The [Mitchell Freeway](/roads/mitchell-freeway) runs through the suburb, with exits at Hutton Street and Scarborough Beach Road. Scarborough Beach Road carries traffic west to [Innaloo](/areas/innaloo) and the coast, and east towards Mount Hawthorn. Main Street is the local cafe and business strip in the residential north-east. Walters Drive leads into the Herdsman business park beside Herdsman Lake, with [Glendalough](/areas/glendalough) to the south and [Woodlands](/areas/woodlands) to the west.

Plenty of the commercial streets are busy with delivery trucks during business hours, so we pick our loading spot carefully to avoid blocking driveways and loading docks.

1. Give us the business name and street number, since many buildings look the same from the road.
2. If you're in a yard or under a shared car park, tell us which gate or entry to use.
3. For dealer moves, have the stock number or VIN handy.
4. On the freeway, hazards on and stay clear of moving traffic.
