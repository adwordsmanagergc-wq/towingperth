## Typical towing jobs in Ferndale

Ferndale is a quiet suburb, built mostly in the late 1960s and 1970s, with the Canning River along its northern side. With established homes and long-time residents, many of our calls here are for everyday breakdowns. A battery that has given up overnight, a car that's leaking coolant on the driveway, or a car that needs to go to a mechanic after its owner has decided it's not safe to drive.

The riverside parks draw walkers, cyclists and anglers, and every so often a car left in a park car park won't start when its owner gets back. Those are quick [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) jobs, and we can take the car home or to your workshop.

Nicholson Road on the eastern side is the busiest road around, and crashes and breakdowns on it are the other main reason we come to Ferndale.

## Ferndale roads and nearby suburbs

[Nicholson Road](/roads/nicholson-road) runs along the eastern edge, separating Ferndale from [Langford](/areas/langford) and linking north to Cannington and south to Thornlie and Canning Vale. Metcalfe Road forms the south-east boundary and connects across to High Road, which runs towards [Riverton](/areas/riverton) and Willetton. Ferndale Crescent loops through the middle of the suburb. To the north, across the river, is [Wilson](/areas/wilson).

## If you're stuck in Ferndale

1. On Nicholson Road, use a side street or bus bay if the car will roll.
2. In a riverside car park, tell us the park name or nearest street.
3. If you've hit a kerb or pothole and the wheel is damaged, let us know so we bring the right gear.
4. Tell us where you want the car to go.
