## Typical Aubin Grove tows

Aubin Grove went from bush and rural blocks to a busy family suburb remarkably quickly, and nearly every household relies on a car. Lyon Road is the main spine, feeding traffic up to the Gibbs Road interchange on the Kwinana Freeway, so a lot of our work is commuters who break down on the way to the freeway or the train. Failed batteries, worn clutches and tyres punctured by building debris on newer streets are all common.

The freeway itself is the riskier job. Between Gibbs Road and Rowley Road traffic is fast, and a stalled car on the shoulder needs moving quickly. For crashes on that stretch we handle the [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) and can bill your insurer so you're not out of pocket.

## Main roads around Aubin Grove

Gibbs Road runs along the northern edge, with [Atwell](/areas/atwell) on the other side, and leads west across the freeway towards [Success](/areas/success) as Russell Road. The Kwinana Freeway forms the western boundary with [Hammond Park](/areas/hammond-park). Gaebler Road heads east into the semi-rural lots of Banjup, while Rowley Road at the southern end connects with the freeway and [Wandi](/areas/wandi) beyond.

Because Aubin Grove backs onto rural land, we also pick up the odd ute or trailer from bigger blocks on its eastern side, and our [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) service can move cars that aren't broken down but need relocating.

## Broken down in Aubin Grove?

1. On Lyon Road, try to stop in a side street or bus bay rather than the traffic lane.
2. If you're on a freeway ramp, tell us whether it's the Gibbs Road or Rowley Road interchange.
3. At home, let us know if the car is in a garage and whether it rolls freely.
4. Keep the keys and any spare with you so the driver can load the car without delay.
