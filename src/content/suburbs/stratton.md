## What Stratton calls usually involve

Stratton is mostly family homes, so many of our jobs here are classic driveway breakdowns. School-run cars with flat batteries, second cars that haven't been driven for weeks and older vehicles with tired starter motors make up a good share of the calls. We'll take the car to your mechanic or [move it](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) wherever it needs to go.

The roads around Stratton bring a different set of problems. Roe Highway brushes the suburb's western edge, and Toodyay Road runs to the north. Both carry fast traffic, and the junctions see their share of rear-end and side-swipe crashes. When that happens, our [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) service will clear the scene and deal with your insurer directly.

## Stratton's roads and surroundings

Farrall Road is the main spine through the suburb, running south towards [Midvale](/areas/midvale) and Morrison Road. O'Connor Road meets it at the shopping centre, and Talbot Road links across towards [Jane Brook](/areas/jane-brook) and the foot of the hills. Directly south, the streets of [Swan View](/areas/swan-view) begin to climb.

Midland's shops, hospital and train station are only a few minutes away, and Toodyay Road along the north gives a quick run out to the Swan Valley and Great Northern Highway.
