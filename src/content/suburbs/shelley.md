## Common callouts in Shelley

Leach Highway defines a lot of what we do in Shelley. It forms the suburb's south-western boundary and climbs onto Shelley Bridge, where traffic squeezes into fewer lanes to cross the river. The merge points on either side catch drivers out in peak hour, and that means rear-end crashes and stalled cars blocking a lane. We handle those [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs and can deal with your insurer directly.

The rest of Shelley is residential, with river views on the northern side and a primary school and neighbourhood shops at Shelley Hub. Our jobs on these streets are usually [breakdowns](/services/breakdown-towing-perth): a dead battery after a holiday, a car that won't start on the school run, or a classic car that needs moving to a specialist.

## Roads and places around Shelley

Leach Highway runs along the south-west and crosses the river north to [Wilson](/areas/wilson) via Shelley Bridge. Riverton Drive East and Barbican Street East carry local traffic along the eastern side, towards [Riverton](/areas/riverton). Fifth Avenue marks part of the western boundary with [Rossmoyne](/areas/rossmoyne), and Tribute Street West leads to the Shelley Hub shops. Across the water to the north are Waterford and Salter Point.
