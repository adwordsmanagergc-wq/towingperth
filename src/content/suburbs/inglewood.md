## What we're called to in Inglewood

Inglewood is an older inner suburb, and the cars here range from daily commuters to well-loved classics parked under carports. Plenty of our jobs are at home: a car that hasn't been started for a few weeks, a dead battery in a tight driveway, or an older car that needs a careful [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) move to a restorer or mechanic. Narrow streets with cars parked on both sides mean we often plan how to get in and out before we arrive.

Beaufort Street brings a different set of callouts. It runs right through the suburb with shops, cafes and restaurants along it, and cars that won't start after an evening out are common. The traffic along Beaufort Street, Walter Road West and Alexander Drive also leads to crashes at the busier intersections, especially in peak hour. Those become [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs, and we can bill your insurer directly.

## Roads and places we cover around Inglewood

Beaufort Street is the spine of the suburb, with the clock tower at the Dundas Road corner a well-known marker. Walter Road West and Central Avenue carry traffic across the suburb, Alexander Drive runs on the western side, and Hamer Parade and North Street are useful connectors through the residential grid.

Neighbouring suburbs include [Mount Lawley](/areas/mount-lawley) to the south, [Maylands](/areas/maylands) to the south-east, [Bayswater](/areas/bayswater) to the east and [Dianella](/areas/dianella) to the north. The Terry Tyzack Aquatic Centre and the Inglewood Civic Centre are handy landmarks to give us when you call.
