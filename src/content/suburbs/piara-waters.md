## Towing in Piara Waters: the common jobs

A lot of Piara Waters households are young families with two cars and long commutes. That means plenty of cars parked on the street in the new parts of the estate, and a steady flow of flat batteries, punctures and cars that won't start on a cold morning. Most of these are simple [breakdown tows](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) to a mechanic of your choice.

The bigger callouts come from the arterial roads around the edge. Nicholson Road and Armadale Road both carry heavy peak traffic, and crashes at the roundabouts and intersections along them are a regular call. We handle the [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) and the paperwork with your insurer.

## Roads we cover around Piara Waters

[Nicholson Road](/roads/nicholson-road) forms the eastern side of Piara Waters, with Harrisdale across the road and [Canning Vale](/areas/canning-vale) to the north. Armadale Road runs along the south, linking west to Jandakot and Cockburn Central and east to Armadale. Warton Road runs down the western side, meets Nicholson Road at the northern tip and carries on north towards [Huntingdale](/areas/huntingdale). Wright Road crosses towards [Southern River](/areas/southern-river), and Piara Drive serves the southern streets. The Piara Nature Reserve takes up a big central area, so some streets on either side are further apart by road than they look on a map.
