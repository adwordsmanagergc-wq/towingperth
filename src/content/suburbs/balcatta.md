## The jobs we see most in Balcatta

Balcatta is split in two. A large part of the suburb is the Balcatta light industrial area, built around Erindale Road and Balcatta Road, full of panel shops, mechanics, wholesalers and trade suppliers. The rest is family housing, much of it built from the 1970s on land that used to be market gardens. That mix means our jobs here range from [container transport](/services/container-transport-perth) for local businesses to moving vehicles in and out of workshops all day.

The roads add plenty more. The Mitchell Freeway runs along the western side, Reid Highway runs along the north, and Wanneroo Road forms the eastern edge. Breakdowns on the freeway ramps and crashes at the big signalised intersections are common, and our [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) crews often bill the insurer directly.

## Roads and connections around Balcatta

Erindale Road is the main way into the industrial area from Reid Highway, and Balcatta Road runs through the middle of it. Amelia Street crosses the residential side past Balcatta Senior High School. [Wanneroo Road](/roads/wanneroo-road) on the east side leads up to [Balga](/areas/balga) and down past [Nollamara](/areas/nollamara), while the [Mitchell Freeway](/roads/mitchell-freeway) on the west gets you to the city or north to Joondalup in minutes. To the south-west, Balcatta runs into [Stirling](/areas/stirling) and its own commercial streets.
