## Mount Claremont jobs we see most

The Perth High Performance Centre on Stephenson Avenue brings a constant stream of swimmers, athletes and spectators. Its car parks are a regular source of flat battery and no-start calls, especially early in the morning after training. Families heading to games or swimming lessons often want the car taken straight to a mechanic so they can get on with the day.

School traffic is the other big factor. John XXIII College and other schools nearby mean morning and afternoon rushes on Alfred Road and the streets around them. Minor bumps and breakdowns during pick-up are common, and we deal with insured crashes through our [insurance towing](/insurance-towing) process.

Graylands Hospital on Brockway Road also has staff and visitor parking, and we're called there now and then for cars that won't start after a long shift.

## Roads and places around Mount Claremont

Alfred Road runs along the southern side towards [Claremont](/areas/claremont), with [Karrakatta](/areas/karrakatta) to the south-east. Brockway Road runs north past Graylands Hospital to meet Underwood Avenue, which leads east towards [Shenton Park](/areas/shenton-park) and Selby Street. Stephenson Avenue starts at Underwood Avenue and runs past the High Performance Centre. Montgomery Avenue is one of the main streets through the residential estates.

[Floreat](/areas/floreat) sits to the north and Swanbourne to the west. For the everyday faults that leave a car stuck in the driveway, our [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) service covers the whole suburb.
