## Why people in Warnbro call us

Warnbro sits between the coast and Ennis Avenue. The Warnbro Centre fronts Warnbro Sound Avenue, and the railway station is beside the junction of Ennis Avenue and Safety Bay Road. Commuters and shoppers make up a good share of our jobs here. Cars left all day at the station come back to flat batteries, and the shopping centre car parks produce the usual run of starter motor and alternator failures.

The traffic side is just as important. Safety Bay Road brings freeway traffic in from Baldivis to meet Ennis Avenue right by the station, and this junction and the roundabouts along Warnbro Sound Avenue are where most of the local bumps happen. We treat those as [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs and can bill your insurer.

Weekends bring families to the Aqua Jetty pools and to the beaches along Warnbro Sound, where a long day out sometimes ends with a car that won't start.

## Roads and places we cover around Warnbro

Warnbro Sound Avenue is the main north to south route, continuing as Read Street towards [Rockingham](/areas/rockingham) and running south past Halliburton Drive into [Port Kennedy](/areas/port-kennedy). Palm Springs Boulevard feeds the residential pockets in between.

Safety Bay Road marks the northern edge, linking the coast at [Safety Bay](/areas/safety-bay) with the freeway in Baldivis. Ennis Avenue, part of National Highway 1, runs through the eastern side and carries traffic towards Mandurah.
