## Common Applecross callouts

Applecross wraps around a bend of the Swan River, and most traffic funnels through Canning Highway on its way to Canning Bridge and the city. That makes the highway our busiest pickup point here. Peak-hour shunts near Riseley Street and the bridge happen often, and a car stopped in a right-turn lane can bring everything to a halt. These are typical [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs.

The precinct around Canning Bridge has filled with apartments and offices, and basement car parks bring their own problems. Flat batteries in underground bays, cars that won't start after weeks without use and low-clearance ramps all mean careful work with the right truck. Down by the water, the riverside reserves draw crowds on sunny afternoons, and we're regularly called to cars that won't start after a long day parked near the yacht club.
