## The jobs we see most in Atwell

Atwell has grown from dairy land into a busy family suburb, and a lot of our work here follows the commute. The Kwinana Freeway runs down the whole western side, and peak-hour stop-start traffic between Armadale Road and Gibbs Road leads to overheating, fuel pump failures and the odd rear-end crash. When that happens we can handle the [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) and talk to your insurer for you.

The other big source of calls is Armadale Road on the northern boundary. It carries heavy freight towards the freeway and Cockburn Central, and breakdowns on it need a quick, safe recovery. Inside the estates, the jobs are more routine: cars that won't start in the driveway, punctured tyres on newer streets and vehicles that need moving to a workshop in Jandakot or Bibra Lake.

## How Atwell connects to the rest of Cockburn

Armadale Road forms the top edge, with [Jandakot](/areas/jandakot) to the north and [Cockburn Central](/areas/cockburn-central) across the freeway interchange. Tapper Road runs down the eastern side of the suburb, next to the semi-rural blocks of Banjup, and Gibbs Road marks the southern edge where [Aubin Grove](/areas/aubin-grove) begins. Across the freeway to the west is [Success](/areas/success), with the Cockburn Gateway shops a short drive away.

The Aubin Grove train station sits on the freeway near Gibbs Road, so its car park is part of our regular round too.
