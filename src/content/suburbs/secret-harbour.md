## Towing jobs around Secret Harbour

Secret Harbour has grown quickly into a large residential suburb at the southern tip of the City of Rockingham, and it has a high share of tradespeople and FIFO workers. That means plenty of utes, 4WDs and work vehicles, and when one of those breaks down in a driveway it usually needs a [tilt tray](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) to get to the mechanic.

The surf beach is the other big draw. The car park by the Surf Life Saving Club and Lagoon Park fills up on summer weekends, and after a long day in the heat it's common for a car to overheat or refuse to start. School pick-up around Comet Bay College adds its own share of minor bumps and flat batteries.

On the edges of the suburb, Mandurah Road carries Highway 1 traffic between Rockingham and Mandurah, and breakdowns there call for a quick, careful pickup.

## Roads and places we cover around Secret Harbour

Mandurah Road forms the eastern boundary of the suburb. Warnbro Sound Avenue runs through the centre and connects north through [Port Kennedy](/areas/port-kennedy) to [Warnbro](/areas/warnbro) and on to Rockingham. Secret Harbour Boulevard, Surf Drive and Bluestone Parkway take you through the estates to the beach, the golf course and the schools.

Heading inland, the quickest way to the Kwinana Freeway is through [Baldivis](/areas/baldivis). To the south, the coast continues into Golden Bay.
