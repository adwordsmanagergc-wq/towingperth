## What we usually do in Viveash

Viveash is mostly homes, built around curving streets that wind down towards the Swan River. Without through traffic, most of our jobs here are home breakdowns: a flat battery on a winter morning, a car that's been sitting since the owner went away, or a project car that needs to go to a workshop. We also [transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) cars for people moving house or selling a vehicle.

When something happens on the main roads just outside the suburb, Viveash residents are often the ones calling. The busy roads around Midland, including Great Northern Highway and Morrison Road, are close by, and minor crashes at the lights on the way home often end with a car limping back into a Viveash driveway. From there we can tow it to a repairer and handle the insurance side through [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth).

## Streets and surroundings in Viveash

Eveline Road and Muriel Street are among the main streets in Viveash, with Sefton Avenue and Bernley Drive looping through the residential streets. Many of the smaller streets are mews and crescents, so it helps to tell us which one you're in.

[Midland](/areas/midland) is just to the south-east, and Great Northern Highway leads north from there past [Herne Hill](/areas/herne-hill) into the Swan Valley. Along the river, the walk trail runs downstream through Woodbridge towards [Guildford](/areas/guildford), and across the river flats are Caversham's vineyards and new estates.

## Stuck in Viveash? Quick tips

1. Give us your street name, as several Viveash streets have similar mews names.
2. If you're near the river, keep the car on firm ground and off the grass verges.
3. In a narrow mews, let us know about parked cars blocking the turn.
4. Have the keys ready and tell us where you'd like the car to go.
