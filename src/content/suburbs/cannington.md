## Cannington callouts: what keeps us busy

Westfield Carousel is the heart of Cannington, and it brings a steady stream of car park callouts. Flat batteries after a long day of shopping, cars that won't come out of park, keys locked in the boot and shopping trolley prangs are all standard here. Multi-deck car parks need some care, and we bring the right truck for the clearance.

Albany Highway runs right past Carousel, and it's one of the busiest roads in Perth. Crashes at the Nicholson Road and Cecil Avenue intersections are a regular source of [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) work, and we'll bill your insurer directly in most cases.

The station car park and the busy bus interchange also see their share of commuter breakdowns, especially in hot weather.

## Roads around Cannington and where they lead

[Albany Highway](/roads/albany-highway) runs north-west to [Bentley](/areas/bentley) and the city, and south-east to [Beckenham](/areas/beckenham) and Armadale. [Nicholson Road](/roads/nicholson-road) starts at Albany Highway and heads south through Lynwood and Thornlie. Manning Road links west to Curtin University and the Kwinana Freeway. Across the railway is [East Cannington](/areas/east-cannington), while the Canning River and its regional park form the south-western edge, with the greyhound track and the Canning showgrounds nearby.

## Broken down in Cannington? Tips that help

1. In Carousel, note the car park name, deck level and nearest store entrance.
2. On Albany Highway, stay in the car with your seatbelt on if you can't reach a safe spot.
3. At the station, give us the car park entry and the row you're in.
4. Let us know where the car needs to go, whether that's home, a workshop or your insurer's repairer.
