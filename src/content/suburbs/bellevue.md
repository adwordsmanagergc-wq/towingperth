## Bellevue jobs we handle most often

Bellevue's mix of highway, industry and housing gives us a wide range of work. The big one is the highway network. Roe Highway cuts straight through the suburb, and the Great Eastern Highway Bypass and [Great Eastern Highway](/roads/great-eastern-highway) both carry heavy trucks heading for the Wheatbelt and Goldfields. When a car stops on any of them, it needs moving quickly.

The industrial side of Bellevue, near the highways, means plenty of work vehicles and machinery. We do [container transport](/services/container-transport-perth) and vehicle moves for businesses here, along with the usual breakdowns of tradies' utes.

In the residential streets, it's often older cars that won't start, or something that's overheated after a run up the hill to Greenmount.

## Roads and nearby suburbs around Bellevue

Clayton Street is the main local road through the residential part of Bellevue. To the north and west, the suburb runs into [Midvale](/areas/midvale) and the Midland centre, while to the east it meets [Koongamia](/areas/koongamia) and the start of the climb up the Darling Scarp. The Helena River and its parkland run along the south, and [Helena Valley](/areas/helena-valley) lies further up the river. The Railway Reserves Heritage Trail starts in Bellevue and heads up into the hills.

3. If the car is overheating after the hill, switch it off and let it cool. Don't open the radiator cap.
4. Tell us the destination, whether it's a Midland workshop or somewhere further away.
