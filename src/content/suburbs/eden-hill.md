## The Eden Hill callouts we get most

Eden Hill is almost entirely family homes, with schools, parks and a few local shops, so most of our work is everyday [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth). Older cars that won't start on a cold morning, failed fuel pumps and cooling problems in summer are all typical. We also tow cars that have been parked for a long time and need to go to a wrecker or a new owner.

The roads around the edges, especially Morley Drive and Lord Street, are where crashes tend to happen. School drop-off and pick-up times along Ivanhoe Street also bring the odd minor bump.

## Roads around Eden Hill

Morley Drive runs along the north side, heading west to [Morley](/areas/morley) and east towards Lord Street. Walter Road East forms the southern side and ends at Lord Street. Lord Street, on the eastern side, runs north towards [Kiara](/areas/kiara) and the Swan Valley, and south into [Bassendean](/areas/bassendean). Ivanhoe Street is one of the main local streets, passing Eden Hill Primary School.

To the north, Eden Hill meets [Lockridge](/areas/lockridge), while Bayswater and Ashfield are a short drive to the south-west. Inside the suburb, the streets are quiet and residential, and a house number is usually all we need to find you.

## What to do if you're stuck in Eden Hill

1. Give us your street name and number, or the nearest corner if you're on a main road.
2. If you're blocking a school entrance or driveway, let us know so we can prioritise the move.
3. Leave the keys with you or in a safe spot if you can't wait with the car.
4. Tell us where the car needs to go, whether it's home or a local workshop.
