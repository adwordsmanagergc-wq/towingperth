## Typical callouts in White Gum Valley

White Gum Valley is a leafy, established suburb of older cottages, post-war homes and newer infill housing, and most of our work here is ordinary household towing. A car that has failed its last start, a project car that needs moving to a new garage, or a family vehicle heading to a workshop for something bigger than a jump start. Our [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) service suits those planned moves.

The streets themselves shape the job. Some are narrow with cars parked on both sides, and the ridge gives a few of them noticeable slopes, so we think about where to load before the truck arrives.

On the boundaries, South Street and Carrington Street carry steady traffic and buses. A breakdown or minor crash at the intersection of these two roads is one of the more common urgent calls in the area.

## Roads and places we cover around White Gum Valley

Stevens Street forms the northern edge, near the Royal Fremantle Golf Club and the Fremantle Golf Course, with [Fremantle](/areas/fremantle) itself to the west of Edmund Street. South Street runs along the southern boundary, separating the suburb from [Beaconsfield](/areas/beaconsfield) and [Hilton](/areas/hilton).

Carrington Street marks the eastern side, and across it lies O'Connor's light industrial area. Inside the suburb, Valley Park and Davies Reserve are handy reference points if you're not sure of the exact street number.
