## What we're called out for in Dianella

Some of the busiest roads in Perth's inner north meet in Dianella. Alexander Drive, Morley Drive and Grand Promenade carry heavy traffic all day, and crashes at the big signalised corners are a regular source of [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) work for us. We usually bill your insurer directly, which saves you paying upfront.

The suburb is mainly established homes with plenty of older family cars, so we also deal with flat batteries, failed alternators and overheating, especially in summer. Dianella Plaza on Grand Promenade is a common spot for car park breakdowns.

## Main roads and nearby suburbs

Alexander Drive runs north to south through Dianella, linking the city with [Mirrabooka](/areas/mirrabooka) and beyond. Morley Drive crosses the suburb from west to east, heading towards [Morley](/areas/morley). Grand Promenade and Dianella Drive carry local traffic, while Walter Road West links down to [Inglewood](/areas/inglewood). [Nollamara](/areas/nollamara) is right next door.

Dianella Regional Open Space, at the corner of Alexander and Morley Drives, has large car parks used for sport and community events. We pick up cars from there too.

## Help for drivers stuck in Dianella

1. On Alexander Drive or Morley Drive, don't stop in the turning lanes. Move to the left side or a side street.
2. Give us the closest intersection and which direction you were travelling.
3. In a car park, mention the entrance you used and the nearest shop or sports ground.
4. If there's been a crash, take photos before the cars are moved.
