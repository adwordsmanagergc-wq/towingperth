## The usual Bull Creek callouts

Bull Creek is a well-established family suburb, but it's bordered on three sides by major roads. The Kwinana Freeway runs along the western edge, and the Leach Highway interchange at the station is one of the busiest junctions in the southern suburbs. Breakdowns on the ramps, rear-end crashes in peak hour and cars that overheat in stop-start traffic all bring us here regularly, and we'll arrange the [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) with your insurer if needed.

South Street on the southern side carries traffic to Murdoch and the hospitals, and gives us a steady stream of jobs too. Inside the suburb, the calls are the everyday kind: school-run breakdowns near All Saints' College, flat batteries after a long weekend and cars that need to go to a mechanic.

## Roads in and around Bull Creek

The freeway separates Bull Creek from [Bateman](/areas/bateman) to the west, and Leach Highway forms the northern edge, with [Brentwood](/areas/brentwood) and [Rossmoyne](/areas/rossmoyne) beyond. South Street marks the southern boundary next to [Leeming](/areas/leeming). Bull Creek Drive runs north to south through the suburb, past the Aviation Heritage Museum, and Parry Avenue is the other main local street.

Because the station sits at the Leach Highway interchange, our drivers can get on and off the freeway quickly to reach you.
