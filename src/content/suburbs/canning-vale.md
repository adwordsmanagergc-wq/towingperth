## Canning Vale towing: the regular jobs

Canning Vale's north-western corner is an industrial area full of warehouses, distribution centres, manufacturers and the Market City fresh produce market. That brings a steady run of jobs for vans, utes and light trucks that break down mid-shift, along with [container transport](/services/container-transport-perth) between warehouses, the port and customers. Roe Highway along the northern edge carries heavy freight, and breakdowns on it need quick, careful handling.

The rest of the suburb is mostly housing, home to tens of thousands of residents. Cars that won't start in the driveway, school-run breakdowns and car park jobs at Livingston Marketplace and The Vale shopping centre make up most of the work here. Ranford Road, South Street and Nicholson Road carry heavy commuter traffic, and crashes at their intersections are common [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs.

## Main roads and neighbours of Canning Vale

Roe Highway forms the northern boundary, with [Riverton](/areas/riverton) and [Parkwood](/areas/parkwood) beyond. Nicholson Road heads north-east towards Cannington, and there's a full rundown of it on our [Nicholson Road page](/roads/nicholson-road). Ranford Road runs east-west through the residential side, covered in our [Ranford Road guide](/roads/ranford-road), and leads east towards [Southern River](/areas/southern-river). South Street runs west towards [Leeming](/areas/leeming) and the freeway, while Warton Road marks the south-eastern edge.
