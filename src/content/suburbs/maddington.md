## What we tow in Maddington

Maddington Central is one of the biggest shopping draws in the City of Gosnells, and its open-air car parks produce a steady trickle of cars that won't start, have a flat tyre on a bad rim or have lost a clutch. Those are quick [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) jobs, often straight to a mechanic a few streets away.

The light industrial area beside Albany Highway and the railway is the other big source of work. Workshops need cars brought in, small businesses need utes and vans moved, and the odd container needs a lift. On the main roads, Albany Highway and Tonkin Highway both see crashes at their busier intersections, and we handle the [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) and insurer paperwork for those.

## Main roads through Maddington

[Albany Highway](/roads/albany-highway) cuts through Maddington on its way between Cannington and Gosnells, with shops and workshops either side. Tonkin Highway runs along the eastern edge of the suburb, with the Kelvin Road and Gosnells Road West interchanges giving access. Burslem Drive and Attfield Street loop around Maddington Central and the train station. Head north-west and you're in [Kenwick](/areas/kenwick), south-west towards [Thornlie](/areas/thornlie), or south into [Gosnells](/areas/gosnells). [Orange Grove](/areas/orange-grove) sits just past Tonkin Highway to the east.

Some of Maddington's older semi-rural blocks, including a few remaining orchards, have long gravel driveways that need a careful approach.
