## The kind of towing we do in Iluka

Iluka is quiet and residential, so callouts here are rarely dramatic. A typical job is a car that won't start after sitting in a beach car park all afternoon, or a family wagon with a failed alternator in a driveway. On weekends, the Iluka Sports Complex is busy with football, cricket, hockey and rugby, and we sometimes collect a car or two that refused to start after the final siren.

Marmion Avenue along the eastern side is where the more serious work happens. It carries a lot of traffic between Joondalup, Ocean Reef and the newer suburbs further north, and its busy intersections, including the one at Shenton Avenue, see their share of crashes. We handle those as [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs and can bill your insurer.

## Roads and places we cover around Iluka

Shenton Avenue marks the southern edge, with [Ocean Reef](/areas/ocean-reef) on the other side. Along the coast, Ocean Reef Road continues north as Burns Beach Road and runs past Iluka's foreshore. Across Marmion Avenue you'll find [Currambine](/areas/currambine), and [Joondalup](/areas/joondalup) city centre is only a few minutes' drive inland.

We cover Beaumaris Beach, the foreshore park with its paths and barbecues, and the sports complex, plus the residential streets in between.
