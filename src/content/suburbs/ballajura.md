## Towing in Ballajura: typical jobs

Lakes, parks and a couple of busy local shopping centres sit at the heart of Ballajura, a large, settled family suburb in the City of Swan. A lot of our calls come from those shops and from driveways: batteries flat after a few days of sitting, cars that overheat on a hot afternoon, and second cars that need [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) to a new home or buyer.

Alexander Drive along the western side is the busiest road around, carrying commuters between the outer north and the city. Rear-end crashes at its traffic lights are a regular job for our [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) crews, and we can organise for your insurer to be billed directly.

## Roads in and around Ballajura

Illawarra Crescent is the suburb's main loop, with North and South sections that link most streets back to Alexander Drive. Marangaroo Drive runs in from [Alexander Heights](/areas/alexander-heights) to the west, and Hepburn Avenue runs along the north towards Landsdale. Head south and you reach the industrial streets of [Malaga](/areas/malaga), while [Beechboro](/areas/beechboro) borders the south-east. Ballajura station on the Ellenbrook line is just over the boundary in Whiteman, and its car park is another spot we're called to.

## Car trouble in Ballajura? Here's what to do

1. If you've stopped on Alexander Drive, try to get into a side street or the left lane and put your hazards on.
2. Tell us North or South when you give an Illawarra Crescent address.
3. Near the lakes and parks, mention the park name so the driver knows which side of the water to come in from.
4. If the car is in a tight driveway or garage, let us know so we bring a truck that fits.
