## What we're called out for in Darch

Three schools cluster around Westport Parade in Darch, and family life here revolves around them. Morning and afternoon school runs mean a lot of short trips, which can be hard on batteries, and that shows up as no-start calls on busy streets. We handle these as routine [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) and take the car home or to your mechanic.

The arterials around Darch are the other side of the picture. Kingsway, Hepburn Avenue and Mirrabooka Avenue carry steady traffic between Wanneroo, Landsdale and the city, and crashes at their intersections and roundabouts come through to our [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) line. We can bill your insurer directly in most cases.

## Main roads around Darch

Hepburn Avenue forms the southern boundary, with [Marangaroo](/areas/marangaroo) on the other side and [Alexander Heights](/areas/alexander-heights) to the south-east. Kingsway heads west to the Kingsway City shopping centre in neighbouring [Madeley](/areas/madeley). Mirrabooka Avenue and Landsdale Road link into Landsdale, and Ashdale Boulevard runs through the suburb past Darch Plaza.

1. Near the schools, keep the car clear of crossings and kiss-and-drive bays if you can push it.
2. On Hepburn Avenue, use the verge or a side street, not the median.
3. Give us the street and nearest landmark, like Darch Plaza or a school name.
4. If the car is parked on a narrow street with cars both sides, let us know so the driver can plan the approach.
