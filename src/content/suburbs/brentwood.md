## Why Brentwood drivers call us

Brentwood is a compact suburb of older post-war homes, many now rebuilt or renovated, and it's bordered by two of the busiest roads south of the river. Leach Highway runs along the southern edge and the Kwinana Freeway along the east, so cars from Brentwood join heavy traffic within a minute or two of leaving home. Breakdowns and crashes on the highway and the freeway ramps nearby make up a big share of our work here, and we deal with [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) and insurer billing for you.

On Brentwood's own streets, the calls are usually simpler. Cars that won't start on a cold morning, older vehicles that have been sitting for months, and commuters stuck at Bull Creek station are all regular jobs.

## Getting around Brentwood

The Kwinana Freeway separates Brentwood from [Rossmoyne](/areas/rossmoyne) on the east. Leach Highway runs along the south, with [Bateman](/areas/bateman) across the road and the Bull Creek station interchange at the corner. Moolyeen Road is the main local link off the highway, and Bateman Road and Adamson Road carry traffic through the residential streets towards [Booragoon](/areas/booragoon) to the west.

Because the freeway and highway are so close, our drivers can usually get in quickly from either direction. If your car just needs moving, not rescuing, we also offer [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth).

## Stuck in Brentwood? Keep it simple

1. If you're on a freeway ramp, pull right off, hazards on, and step behind the barrier.
2. In the residential streets, park clear of driveways so the truck can get alongside.
3. Give us the street name and the nearest cross street, or the station car park row if you're there.
4. Tell us if the car is in a garage with low clearance.
