## What gets us out to Heathridge

Heathridge is a settled residential suburb, so a lot of what we do here is straightforward breakdown work. Cars that won't start in the morning, fuel pumps that give out, a dead battery after the car's been sitting for a week. We pick them up from driveways and kerbsides and take them to your mechanic, or to a workshop in nearby Joondalup if you'd rather.

The roads around the edge are another matter. Ocean Reef Road and Marmion Avenue both carry steady traffic between the coast and the city centre, and their intersections see their share of crashes. On the eastern side, the Mitchell Freeway is close enough that freeway breakdowns near Hodges Drive and Ocean Reef Road are regular jobs for us. When there's damage, we handle it as [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) and bill your insurer.

## Roads and places we cover around Heathridge

Hodges Drive runs along the northern boundary towards [Joondalup](/areas/joondalup), and Ocean Reef Road along the south. Marmion Avenue separates Heathridge from [Ocean Reef](/areas/ocean-reef) to the west, while the [Mitchell Freeway](/roads/mitchell-freeway) divides it from [Edgewater](/areas/edgewater) to the east.

Inside the suburb, Caridean Street leads to the local shopping centre, and Heathridge Park and the leisure centre on Sail Terrace draw weekend sport traffic. Craigie and Mullaloo are both a short drive south and west.
