## Why Wilson drivers call us

Leach Highway is the biggest source of our work in Wilson. It crosses the suburb on its way to Shelley Bridge, and the lane merge before the river crossing is a known congestion point. Rear-end crashes and stalled cars in the queue are regular jobs, and we handle them as [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) with insurer billing where possible.

The rest of Wilson is a mix of older homes, units and student rentals, thanks to Curtin University being so close. Students tend to keep older cars running on a tight budget, which means plenty of [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) for flat batteries, overheating and worn clutches. The parks along the river, including Kent Street Weir and Castledare, also bring weekend visitors whose cars sometimes refuse to start at the end of the day.

## Roads and places we cover around Wilson

Leach Highway runs through Wilson and over Shelley Bridge to [Shelley](/areas/shelley) and [Riverton](/areas/riverton). Manning Road forms part of the northern boundary with [Bentley](/areas/bentley), and Centenary Avenue links Leach Highway with Manning Road near Curtin University. Bungaree Road, Fern Road and Armstrong Road carry most local traffic. To the east is [Cannington](/areas/cannington), with Ferndale across the river to the south.
