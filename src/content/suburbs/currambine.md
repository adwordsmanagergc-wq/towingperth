## Towing in Currambine: the usual callouts

Built in the 1990s around its train station, Currambine owes a lot of our callouts to that station. Commuters drive in from Kinross, Iluka and further north, park all day and sometimes come back to a dead battery or a car that won't crank. We tow those cars home or to a workshop, often in the evening rush.

Currambine Central draws its own crowd, and car park breakdowns there are routine [breakdown towing](/services/breakdown-towing-perth) jobs for us. Out on the edges, the [Mitchell Freeway](/roads/mitchell-freeway) on the east side and Burns Beach Road on the north carry fast traffic, and freeway crashes near the Burns Beach Road interchange are among our more urgent callouts.

## Roads and nearby suburbs

Connolly Drive runs north to south through Currambine and Moore Drive runs east to west, splitting the suburb into four quarters. Moore Drive links east into [Joondalup](/areas/joondalup), while Burns Beach Road separates Currambine from [Kinross](/areas/kinross). Marmion Avenue on the west side borders [Iluka](/areas/iluka), and Shenton Avenue on the south leads past Joondalup Resort's golf course, part of which sits inside Currambine.
