## Why people in Alexander Heights call us

Alexander Heights is a residential pocket wedged between two busy roads. Alexander Drive runs down its eastern edge and Mirrabooka Avenue down its western side, and both carry heavy commuter traffic between the outer north and the city. Peak hour on these roads brings nose-to-tail crashes at the traffic lights, and those jobs become [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) where we can usually deal with your insurer directly.

The other steady source of work is the shopping centre on Mirrabooka Avenue. Cars that sat in the sun all afternoon, batteries that finally gave up and keys that won't turn all end up as callouts from that car park. In the streets behind it, we often move cars that have been off the road for a while and now need to go to a workshop or a new owner.

## Getting around Alexander Heights

Marangaroo Drive runs along the southern edge of the suburb, linking across to [Marangaroo](/areas/marangaroo) and [Girrawheen](/areas/girrawheen) on one side and [Ballajura](/areas/ballajura) on the other. Hepburn Avenue marks the northern edge, where Alexander Heights meets [Landsdale](/areas/landsdale) and Darch. That grid of arterials means most addresses here are only a minute or two from a main road, which helps when we're lining up a tilt tray.

If your car needs to go further than the local mechanic, our [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) service can move it anywhere in Perth.

## Stuck in Alexander Heights? Do this first

1. On Alexander Drive or Mirrabooka Avenue, get into the left lane or a slip lane if the car will still roll, then switch on your hazards.
2. If you're in a residential street, check the car isn't across someone's driveway or a footpath ramp before you leave it.
3. Note the nearest intersection or house number. Many crescents here loop back on themselves, so a cross street helps the driver.
4. Have your rego and insurance details handy if another car was involved.
