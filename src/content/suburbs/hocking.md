## Why people in Hocking call for a tow

Hocking is mostly family homes on curving streets, and the bulk of our callouts come from those driveways and verges. Batteries die, clutches wear out, a coolant hose splits in the summer heat. We load the car onto a tilt tray and take it wherever you want it to go, often only a short drive away in Wangara or Wanneroo.

The rest of our Hocking work happens on the main roads around it. Wanneroo Road along the western boundary is one of the busiest roads in the north, with big trucks and commuter traffic mixed together. Lenore Road on the east side is now a dual carriageway between Kemp Street and Elliot Road, and the traffic it carries brings the occasional intersection crash. Those are [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs, and we'll talk to your insurer directly.

## Roads and places we cover around Hocking

The suburb is framed by four roads: Elliot Road to the north, Lenore Road to the east, East Road to the south and [Wanneroo Road](/roads/wanneroo-road) to the west. Over Elliot Road is the older town centre of [Wanneroo](/areas/wanneroo), and south of East Road you're in [Pearsall](/areas/pearsall). The industrial estate at [Wangara](/areas/wangara) is a short hop away to the south-east.

Within Hocking, Wyatt Grove Shopping Centre on Gungurru Avenue is the local shopping spot, and Greenfields Park on Elliot Road sees weekend sports traffic.
