## What brings us to Belmont

Great Eastern Highway runs along the north-western edge of Belmont, and it's one of the busiest roads in the eastern suburbs. With traffic heading to the airport, the city and the hills, rear-end crashes and breakdowns in peak hour are common. These are typical [accident towing](/services/accident-towing-perth) jobs where we'll usually bill your insurer directly.

Belmont is also a big residential suburb, with older homes and newer units on Alexander Road and Belmont Avenue. That means flat batteries, worn starter motors and cars that need moving to a workshop. With the airport so close, we also get calls from people whose car won't start when they get back from a trip.

## Main roads and areas near Belmont

[Great Eastern Highway](/roads/great-eastern-highway) carries traffic towards the city in one direction and towards the airport and Midland in the other. Abernethy Road runs south-east from the highway towards [Cloverdale](/areas/cloverdale) and the industrial area in [Kewdale](/areas/kewdale). Belmont Avenue marks the boundary with Cloverdale, and Alexander Road heads west towards [Rivervale](/areas/rivervale).

To the north, Belmont meets Ascot and the river, while [Redcliffe](/areas/redcliffe) and the airport precinct sit to the east.
