## What we're called out for in Banksia Grove

Banksia Grove has grown quickly from bushland into one of the bigger family suburbs in the City of Wanneroo, and plenty of our jobs come from young families and first home owners. School runs to Joseph Banks Secondary College and the primary schools mean early morning flat batteries and cars that won't start just when everyone needs to leave. Cars that need moving to a new owner or a workshop are another regular [vehicle transport](/services/vehicle-transport-perth) job.

The roads out of the suburb are long and fairly quick. [Joondalup Drive](/roads/joondalup-drive) runs west towards Joondalup, Pinjar Road heads south through [Tapping](/areas/tapping), and Neaves Road continues east into semi-rural land. A breakdown on any of these at night can leave you well away from shops and lights, so a tow is often the only sensible option.

## Roads and places around Banksia Grove

Most traffic funnels through the corner of Joondalup Drive and Pinjar Road, next to the Banksia Grove Village shops, with Joseph Banks Boulevard running through the retail and school precinct. Discovery Park, with its stream and playground, sits within the suburb and is a handy landmark if you're nearby. To the south, [Ashby](/areas/ashby) and [Wanneroo](/areas/wanneroo) are only a short drive down Pinjar Road and Wanneroo Road.

## Stuck in Banksia Grove? Steps to take

1. On Joondalup Drive or Neaves Road, pull fully onto the verge and switch on your hazards. Neaves Road in particular has long rural stretches.
2. If you're on a brand new street, send us a location pin from your phone.
3. Use the shopping village, Discovery Park or a school as a reference point.
4. Let us know if there are kids or pets in the car so we can prioritise the job.
